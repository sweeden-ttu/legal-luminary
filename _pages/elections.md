## Bell County Elections Office

The county's early-voting clerk is Dr. Desi Roberts, Bell County Elections Administrator; the office's mailing address is P.O. Box 1629, Belton, TX 76513, its telephone number is 254-933-5774, and its email is Elections@BellCounty.Texas.gov ([City of Killeen election order, Aug. 4, 2026](https://www.killeentexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14296/Order-of-Special-Election-1132026-PDF)). ## Rules at the polling place (Texas Election Code)

- **Hours.** On election day the polls open at 7 a.m. and close at 7 p.m. ([Election Code §41.031](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.41.htm#41.031)).
- **Photo ID.** Acceptable identification is a Texas driver's license, election identification certificate or personal identification card issued by DPS; a U.S. military ID card with a photograph; a U.S. citizenship certificate with a photograph; a U.S. passport book or card; or a DPS license to carry a handgun. Except for the citizenship certificate, the document must be unexpired or have expired no earlier than four years before it is presented, and a voter 70 or older may use an expired form of identification if it is otherwise valid ([Election Code §63.0101](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.63.htm#63.0101)).
- **Voting order priority.** Election officers must accept a voter with a mobility problem that substantially impairs the ability to walk ahead of others who arrived earlier, and a person assisting that voter may also be given priority at the voter's request; notice of this rule must be posted at each polling place entrance ([Election Code §63.0015](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.63.htm#63.0015)).
- **Curbside voting.** If a voter is physically unable to enter the polling place without personal assistance, an election officer must, on request, deliver a ballot to the voter at the polling place entrance or curb; each polling place must reserve a parking space for curbside voting with a sign giving a contact number or call button ([Election Code §64.009](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.64.htm#64.009)).
- **Electioneering.** Loitering or electioneering for or against any candidate, measure or party within 100 feet of an outside door through which voters enter the polling place, or within 20 feet of a curbside-voting parking space, is a Class C misdemeanor ([Election Code §61.003](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.61.htm#61.003)).
- **Phones and cameras.** A person may not use a wireless communication device in a room where voting is taking place, and may not record images or sound within 100 feet of a voting station; the presiding judge may require the device to be turned off or the person to leave. Election officers on duty and employees working at the building are exempt ([Election Code §61.014](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/el/htm/el.61.htm#61.014)).
- **Weapons.** Carrying a firearm or other listed weapon on the premises of a polling place on election day or while early voting is in progress is an offense under the Penal Code, subject to that chapter's exceptions ([Penal Code §46.03(a)(2)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/pe/htm/pe.46.htm#46.03)).
