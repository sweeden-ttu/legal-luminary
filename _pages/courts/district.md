# Bell County District Courts

<p class="intro-text">Six state district courts sit at the Bell County Justice Center in Belton. District judges are elected countywide on a party ballot to four-year terms; the 27th District also covers Lampasas County. Vacancies are filled by the Governor until the next general election. Three seats — the 169th, 264th and 478th — are on the November 3, 2026 ballot, each with an unopposed Republican nominee.</p>
