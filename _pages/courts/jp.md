# Justices of the Peace

<p class="intro-text">Bell County has six justice of the peace seats (<a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_1/index.php">Bell County JP Pct 1</a>, <a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_2/index.php">Pct 2</a>, <a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_3_place_1/index.php">Pct 3 Pl 1</a>, <a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_3_place_2/index.php">Pct 3 Pl 2</a>, <a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_4_place_1/index.php">Pct 4 Pl 1</a>, <a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_4_place_2/index.php">Pct 4 Pl 2</a>), elected by precinct to four-year terms (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm#5.18">Tex. Const. art. V §18</a>). JP courts handle evictions (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/pr/htm/pr.24.htm#24.004">Prop. Code §24.004</a>), civil cases up to $20,000 (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.27.htm#27.031">Gov't Code §27.031</a>), fine-only criminal offenses (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cr/htm/cr.4.htm#4.11">Code Crim. Proc. art. 4.11</a>) and truancy (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/fa/htm/fa.65.htm#65.004">Family Code §65.004</a>); justices of the peace are magistrates (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cr/htm/cr.2a.htm#2A.151">Code Crim. Proc. art. 2A.151</a>) and conduct death inquests in counties without a medical examiner (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cr/htm/cr.49.htm#49.04">art. 49.04</a>). Four seats were on the November 3, 2026 ballot; Precinct 4, Place 2 (Killeen) was the only contested judicial race in the county (<a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/departments/elections/ballot_samples.php">Bell County ballot samples</a>; <a href="https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/cox-and-gonzalez-participate-in-justice-of-the-peace-forum-ahead-of-nov-3-election/article_f3226aea-4314-4639-9f1d-804bfbe9c839.html">KDH</a>). Democrat Jessica A. Gonzalez won Precinct 4, Place 2 over Republican Beatrice "Bea" Cox, and Republicans Patrick Patterson (Precinct 1), Michael Tice (Precinct 2) and Larry Wilkey (Precinct 3, Place 2) were elected without opposition, according to unofficial returns (<a href="https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/">Bell County Elections</a>; <a href="https://kdhnews.com/news/local/killeen-mayor-pro-tem-jessica-gonzalez-steamrolls-to-victory-in-bell-county-jp-race/article_a4e03829-e513-4185-a9c0-547fe8c4823e.html">KDH</a>).</p>
