# Justices of the Peace

<p class="intro-text">Bell County elects six justices of the peace by precinct to four-year terms. JP courts handle evictions, small claims up to $20,000, Class C misdemeanors, truancy and magistration, and they pronounce deaths outside hospitals. No law license is required. Four seats are on the November 3, 2026 ballot; Precinct 4, Place 2 (Killeen) is the only contested judicial race in the county.</p>
