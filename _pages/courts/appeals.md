# Courts of Appeals

<p class="intro-text">Bell County is in the Third Court of Appeals District, so its appeals go to the <strong>Third Court of Appeals</strong> in Austin, a six-justice court elected by the voters of 24 counties (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.22.htm#22.201">Gov't Code §22.201(d)</a>; <a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.22.htm#22.216">§22.216(c)</a>; <a href="https://www.txcourts.gov/3rdcoa/">Texas Courts</a>). The <strong>Fifteenth Court of Appeals</strong>, created by the Legislature in 2023 with its first term beginning September 1, 2024, has statewide civil intermediate appellate jurisdiction over cases involving state agencies and officers and over appeals from the business court; its district is every county in Texas, so its justices are elected statewide (<a href="https://www.txcourts.gov/15thcoa/">Texas Courts</a>; <a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.22.htm#22.220">Gov't Code §22.220(d)</a>; <a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.25a.htm#25A.007">§25A.007</a>; <a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/gv/htm/gv.22.htm#22.201">§22.201(p)</a>). Justices serve six-year terms (<a href="https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm#5.6">Tex. Const. art. V §6</a>). Four of these seats were on the November 3, 2026 ballot (<a href="https://www.bellcountytx.com/departments/elections/ballot_samples.php">Bell County ballot samples</a>). Democrats unseated all three Republican justices of the Fifteenth Court (<a href="https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html">Texas Secretary of State</a>; <a href="https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/">Texas Tribune</a>), and Third Court Chief Justice Darlene Byrne (D) was re-elected over Cory Liu (R) across the 24-county district, although Liu carried Bell County, according to unofficial returns (<a href="https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html">Texas Secretary of State</a>; <a href="https://www.ksat.com/vote-2026/2026/10/30/judicial-election-results-in-texas-and-san-antonio-area-on-nov-3-2026/">KSAT</a>; <a href="https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/">Bell County Elections</a>).</p>
