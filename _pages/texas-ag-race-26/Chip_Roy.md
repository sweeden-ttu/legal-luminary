# Chip Roy and the 2026 Texas Attorney General race

**Chip Roy** (Republican) is a member of the United States House of Representatives from Texas's 21st Congressional District; he assumed that office on January 3, 2019 and his term ends January 3, 2027 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy)). In 2026 he ran for **Attorney General of Texas**: he finished second in the March 3 Republican primary with 31.6% and lost the May 26 runoff to **Mayes Middleton** ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy)). The general election is November 3, 2026 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_Attorney_General_election,_2026)). This page is informational only and does not endorse any candidate.

## Background and education

According to [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy), Roy was born in Bethesda, Maryland, in 1972. He earned a bachelor's degree in commerce and an M.S. in management information systems from the University of Virginia (1994 and 1995) and a J.D. from the University of Texas (2003) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy)).

## Career before Congress

Ballotpedia lists Roy's prior experience as federal prosecutor and special assistant U.S. attorney in the Eastern District of Texas; federal banking analyst; First Assistant Attorney General of Texas; chief of staff to U.S. Sen. Ted Cruz; senior advisor to Gov. Rick Perry; staff director of Sen. John Cornyn's leadership office; senior counsel on the Senate Judiciary Committee; and vice president of strategy at the Texas Public Policy Foundation ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy)).

## U.S. House

Roy is not running for re-election to the House in 2026; Ballotpedia lists him among withdrawn candidates for the district's March 3 Republican primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Chip_Roy)). His official House site is [roy.house.gov](https://roy.house.gov/).

## 2026 campaign for Attorney General

In the March 3, 2026 Republican primary, Middleton led with 39.1%, Roy had 31.6%, Joan Huffman 15.1% and Aaron Reitz 14.2%; in the May 26 runoff Middleton defeated Roy, 757,625 votes (55.2%) to 614,009 (44.8%) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_Attorney_General_election,_2026)). His campaign website is [chiproy.com](https://www.chiproy.com/).
