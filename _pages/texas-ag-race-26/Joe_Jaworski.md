# Joe Jaworski and the 2026 Texas Attorney General race

**Joe Jaworski** (Democrat) is an attorney and former mayor of **Galveston, Texas** ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski)). In 2026 he ran for **Attorney General of Texas**: he finished second in the March 3 Democratic primary and lost the May 26 runoff to **Nathan Johnson** ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski)). The general election is November 3, 2026 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_Attorney_General_election,_2026)). This page is informational only and does not endorse any candidate.

## Background and education

According to [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski), Jaworski resides in Galveston. He earned a bachelor's degree in Spanish language and literature from Davidson College (1984) and a J.D. from the University of Texas School of Law (1991). Ballotpedia lists his career experience as owner of the Jaworski Law Firm and attorney with Griggs and Harrison, P.C., and Simpson, Beeton, Finegan & Jaworski, L.L.P. ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski)).

## Public service

Ballotpedia describes Jaworski as a former mayor of Galveston who lost his bid for re-election after a runoff ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski)). He also ran for attorney general in 2022, losing the Democratic runoff to Rochelle Garza, 62.7% to 37.3% ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Jaworski)).

## 2026 campaign for Attorney General

In the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary, Nathan Johnson led with 48.1%, Jaworski had 26.4% and Tony Box 25.5%; in the May 26 runoff Johnson defeated Jaworski, 334,199 votes (60.5%) to 218,224 (39.5%) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_Attorney_General_election,_2026)).
