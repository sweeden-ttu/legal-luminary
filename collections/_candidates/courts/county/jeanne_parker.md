profile_summary: |
  Jeanne Parker is a former judge of Bell County Court at Law No. 1. The Texas Office of Court Administration lists her as the court's judge with an assumed-office date of Jan. 1, 2015 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1460596/county-courts-at-law-statutory-probate-courts.pdf)).

  In the March 4, 2014 Republican primary, Parker, then an assistant county attorney who prosecuted Child Protective Services cases, unofficially defeated Ken Valka 6,042 to 5,905 (50.57%) for the open County Court at Law No. 1 seat; no Democrat filed for the seat, which opened when Judge Edward Johnson, who had held it since 1987, retired ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/politics/trudo-defeats-parker-in-judge-s-race/article_63f1238a-a423-11e3-8e23-001a4bcf6878.html)). Before the primary, the Herald reported that Parker, 65, of Belton, had lived in Bell County for 20 years and had been an assistant county attorney since 2009; her earlier work included heading the family, law and criminal divisions at Dan Corbin & Associates (2005–2006) and 18 months advising the Afghan Ministry of the Interior (2006–2007) ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/politics/two-vie-for-bell-county-court-at-law-no-1-position/article_8e6cd602-96c9-11e3-8aa1-001a4bcf6878.html)).

  She was sworn in again as County Court at Law No. 1 judge at the Bell County Commissioners Court's first meeting of 2019 ([101.7 Jack FM](https://1017jackfm.com/new-leadership-makes-history-as-bell-county-officials-are-sworn-in/)). The Jan. 3, 2023 Commissioners Court agenda listed the oath of office for County Court at Law No. 1 Judge Paul A. Motz ([Bell County Commissioners Court via Swagit](https://bellcountytx.new.swagit.com/videos/247771)).
