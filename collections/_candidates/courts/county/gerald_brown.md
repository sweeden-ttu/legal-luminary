profile_summary: |
  Gerald M. Brown is a former judge of Bell County Court at Law No. 3 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Gerald_M._Brown)). The Texas Office of Court Administration's 2008 judicial directory lists him as the judge of County Court at Law No. 3, alongside Edward S. Johnson (No. 1) and John Mischtian (No. 2) ([Texas Courts, 2008 Judicial Directory via UNT Web Archive](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20091005155756/http:/www.courts.state.tx.us/pubs/2008JudicialDirectory/TrialCourtJudges-PersonnelbyCo.pdf)).

  The court's current judge, Rebecca DePew, won the seat in the November 2010 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_county_court_judicial_elections,_2010_(A-B))) and assumed office Jan. 1, 2011 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1460596/county-courts-at-law-statutory-probate-courts.pdf)). In 2025, County Court at Law No. 3 candidate John Gauntt Jr. told the Killeen Daily Herald that the court has had only two presiding judges, Brown and DePew ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/fourth-generation-lawyer-launches-campaign-for-bell-county-court-at-law-3/article_ea7e20e4-27c6-54df-877b-ca20a9b769e2.html)).
