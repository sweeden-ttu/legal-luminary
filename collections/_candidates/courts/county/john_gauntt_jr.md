profile_summary: |
  John Gauntt Jr. is a Bell County prosecutor who entered the 2026 race for judge of Bell County Court at Law No. 3, the county's juvenile court, as a Republican. He told the Killeen Daily Herald he has about 24 years of experience prosecuting juvenile cases, that he is a fourth-generation Bell County lawyer, that he has trained police officers, probation officers and attorneys across Texas in juvenile law, and that he served six years on the State Bar of Texas Juvenile Law Section board, the last two as an officer. Bell County Attorney Jim Nichols endorsed him. Gauntt said the court has had only two presiding judges, Gerald Brown and current Judge Rebecca DePew, and that DePew is retiring ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/fourth-generation-lawyer-launches-campaign-for-bell-county-court-at-law-3/article_ea7e20e4-27c6-54df-877b-ca20a9b769e2.html)).

  His campaign website describes him as a fourth-generation Bell County lawyer and a prosecutor for about 24 to 25 years, says juvenile law has been "at the heart of my work for more than two decades," and says his wife served two terms on the Belton City Council ([campaign website](https://johngaunttjr.com/about)).

  **2026 election:** The Herald listed Gauntt as the uncontested Republican candidate for County Court at Law seat No. 3 in the March 3, 2026 primary ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/locals-vying-for-county-seats-in-primary-elections/article_6337d52a-83e9-5b75-8efe-8f6d35b2a54b.html)). He is the only candidate for the seat on the Nov. 3, 2026 general election ballot ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)); the Indivisible CenTex voter guide likewise lists no Democratic candidate ([2026 Bell County Voter Guide](https://sites.google.com/view/bellcounty2026/other-countywide-races)).
