profile_summary: |
  Susan Criss is listed by Bell County as an Associate District Judge of the Bell County District Courts, one of two such judges alongside Brittany Darby ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/district_court_associate_judge/index.php)). A 2025 biography published by the National Association of Women Judges describes her as Associate Criminal District Judge for Bell County and says she took the oath of office on July 7, 2025 ([National Association of Women Judges](https://www.nawj.org/uploads/files/bios/crisssusanbio2025.pdf)).

  Before coming to Bell County she was a district judge in Galveston for about fifteen years ([National Association of Women Judges](https://www.nawj.org/uploads/files/bios/crisssusanbio2025.pdf)). Ballotpedia records her as judge of the Texas 212th District Court from 1999 until she left office on December 6, 2013, re-elected in 2010 as a Democrat ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Susan_Criss)).
