profile_summary: |
  Christopher Cornish is listed by Bell County as the Presiding Associate Judge of Centex Child Protection Court #1, 3rd Administrative Judicial Region ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/centex_child_protection_court/index.php)). The Texas Office of Court Administration's December 2019 map of child protection courts lists Judge Chris Cornish for Centex CPC No. 1 in Region 3 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1445119/child-protection-courts-map.pdf)), and the court's posted docket for October 7, 2026 names him as the judge ([Texas Courts, Child Protection Dockets](https://cpdockets.txcourts.gov/Reports/14/Bell/14-Bell-20261007.pdf)).

  The Killeen Daily Herald reported that Chris Cornish was sworn in as judge of the Centex Child Protection Court by 146th District Judge Jack Jones, replacing Judge Charles Van Orden, who retired after 18 years on the court; Cornish was appointed by Billy Ray Stubblefield, presiding judge of the Third Administrative Judicial Region ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/cornish-sworn-in-as-child-protection-court-judge/article_02a9470e-6e57-5cbb-ad87-753b2a34c72d.html)). The same report said he had practiced law since 2008 at his own firm, the Cornish Law Firm, had served two years as the city of Belton's prosecutor, earned his law degree from Creighton University in 2008 and a bachelor's degree in criminology from the University of Northern Iowa, and served nine years in the Army ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/cornish-sworn-in-as-child-protection-court-judge/article_02a9470e-6e57-5cbb-ad87-753b2a34c72d.html)).
