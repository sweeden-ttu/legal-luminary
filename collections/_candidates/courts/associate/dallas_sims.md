  Dallas Sims is listed by Bell County as the Presiding Associate Judge of Centex Child Protection Court #2, 3rd Administrative Judicial Region ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/centex_child_protection_court/index.php)). The Texas Office of Court Administration's December 2019 map of child protection courts lists Judge Dallas Sims for Centex CPC No. 2 in Region 3 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1445119/child-protection-courts-map.pdf)), and the court's posted docket for June 16, 2026 names Dallas J. Sims as the judge ([Texas Courts, Child Protection Dockets](https://cpdockets.txcourts.gov/Reports/46/Bell/46-Bell-20260616.pdf)).

  In its report on Chris Cornish's swearing-in to Centex Child Protection Court 1, the Killeen Daily Herald said Sims had been sworn in the previous month as the other new judge on the Centex Child Protection Court, and that both were appointed by Billy Ray Stubblefield, presiding judge of the Third Administrative Judicial Region ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/cornish-sworn-in-as-child-protection-court-judge/article_02a9470e-6e57-5cbb-ad87-753b2a34c72d.html)).
