profile_summary: |
  The Bell County Commissioners Court appointed Anthony Woolstrum to the newly created position of Bell County magistrate at its first meeting of 2026; the Killeen Daily Herald described him as a Bell County attorney whose practice focuses on child welfare and reported that he had worked with judges to prepare for the role ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-commissioners-court-first-magistrate-appointed-pio-returns-esd-3-gets-another-try/article_de3337ec-b852-50e6-bca5-60499ba553e7.html)). The position was created by Senate Bill 251, which the Herald reported took effect June 20; County Judge David Blackburn noted the critical role of magistration in the criminal justice system, and the Herald described the magistrate's duties as administering legal warnings to those accused of crimes and setting bail and bond conditions, among other duties under state law ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-commissioners-court-first-magistrate-appointed-pio-returns-esd-3-gets-another-try/article_de3337ec-b852-50e6-bca5-60499ba553e7.html)). The news aggregator Ground News dates the Herald report to January 6, 2026 ([Ground News](https://ground.news/article/bell-county-commissioners-court-first-magistrate-appointed-pio-returns-esd-3-gets-another-try)).

  Senate Bill 251 (89th Legislature, 2025) added Subchapter EE, "Bell County Criminal Magistrates," to Chapter 54 of the Texas Government Code; under it the Bell County Commissioners Court selects the magistrates, who serve at its pleasure, must have served as a justice of the peace or municipal judge or have been licensed to practice law in Texas for at least two years, hold concurrent criminal jurisdiction with the county's justice of the peace courts, and give preference to Article 15.17 magistration duties, bail and release-condition hearings, and appointment of counsel ([Texas Senate, S.B. 251 bill analysis](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/analysis/html/SB00251F.htm)).
