profile_summary: |
  Fancy H. Jezek was the first judge of the 426th District Court in Bell County. Gov. Rick Perry appointed her in 2007, the year the court was created, and she took her oath in February 2007 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jezek-to-retire-from-426th-district-court-thursday/article_3a381f7e-3029-58c2-aa2d-94d3b2a32415.html)). She was re-elected without opposition in 2012; in the 2016 Republican primary she defeated Jeff Parker with 54.44% of the vote and then won the November 2016 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Fancy_Jezek)). She retired on April 30, 2020 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Fancy_Jezek)), after about 13 years on a bench that handled family law and felony criminal cases ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jezek-to-retire-from-426th-district-court-thursday/article_3a381f7e-3029-58c2-aa2d-94d3b2a32415.html)).

  She graduated from Baylor Law School in 1980 and began practicing law in Bell County that year ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jezek-to-retire-from-426th-district-court-thursday/article_3a381f7e-3029-58c2-aa2d-94d3b2a32415.html)). Ballotpedia lists her undergraduate degree as from the University of Mary Hardin-Baylor and her prior practice as a partner at Holbrook, Jezek and Griffin ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Fancy_Jezek)).

  Three Republicans ran in 2020 to replace her ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/three-republicans-vying-for-426th-district-court-judgeship/article_d9f35960-4f74-11ea-9617-2790fe7a8695.html)); Gov. Greg Abbott appointed the nominee, Steve Duskie, to the court on July 23, 2020 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-gallagher-to-96th-judicial-district-court)).
