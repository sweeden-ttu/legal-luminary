profile_summary: |
  Gordon G. Adams was judge of the 169th District Court in Bell County from 2000 to 2021 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Gordon_G._Adams)). Gov. George W. Bush appointed him to the bench in June 2000 ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/adams-will-seek-final-term)), and he was elected to the court six times ([Texas House of Representatives, H.R. 911](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/87R/billtext/html/HR00911F.htm)). He served as local administrative district judge for Bell County beginning in December 2012 ([H.R. 911](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/87R/billtext/html/HR00911F.htm)). He retired on July 2, 2021, after more than two decades on the bench, and continued as a part-time retired senior district judge for the Third Administrative Judicial Region ([H.R. 911](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/87R/billtext/html/HR00911F.htm)). Gov. Greg Abbott appointed Cari Starritt-Burnett to succeed him ([KWTX](https://www.kwtx.com/2021/08/06/governor-appoints-2-judges-fill-vacancies-central-texas-courts)); Adams administered her ceremonial oath and noted she is the court's first woman judge ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/new-judge-sworn-in-starritt-burnett-replaces-adams-on-169th-court-bench/article_6053b78f-e867-542a-8433-e56b08c843f6.html)).

  Before his appointment he spent more than eight years as Title IV-D court master for the Third Administrative Judicial Region, presiding over child support dockets in six counties ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/adams-will-seek-final-term)). He served on the Killeen City Council from 1982 to 1984, as Killeen mayor pro tempore from 1983 to 1984, and on the Killeen Independent School District board of trustees for nearly a decade, including as its president from 1989 to 1993 ([H.R. 911](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/87R/billtext/html/HR00911F.htm)). He holds a bachelor's degree from Southwest Texas State University (now Texas State University) and a law degree from the University of Texas School of Law ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/adams-will-seek-final-term)).
