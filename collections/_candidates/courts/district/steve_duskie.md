profile_summary: |
  Steve Duskie is the judge of the 426th Judicial District Court in Bell County ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/426th_district_court/index.php)). In 2020 he was one of three Republicans who ran to replace retiring Judge Fancy Jezek; no Democrat ran for the seat ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/three-republicans-vying-for-426th-district-court-judgeship/article_d9f35960-4f74-11ea-9617-2790fe7a8695.html)). He led the March 3, 2020 primary with 41% of the vote over Jeff Parker (36%) and Wade Faulkner (23%) ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/duskie-parker-to-vie-for-426th-judge-runoff/article_89f90810-3e0d-5b25-bd1a-5210e68d751d.html)), won the July runoff with 60% of the vote, and faced no Democratic opponent in November 2020 ([Texas Lawyer](https://law.com/texaslawyer/2020/07/24/3-republicans-rise-to-judge-in-texas-but-2-must-still-face-elections)). Gov. Greg Abbott appointed him to the court on July 23, 2020, for a term set to expire Dec. 31, 2020, or until his successor was elected and qualified ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-gallagher-to-96th-judicial-district-court)). He was the only candidate for the seat in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election, receiving 87,272 votes ([Bell County official results](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/bellcountytx/elections/Results/20241105_Summary_Results.pdf)). District judges in Texas serve four-year terms ([Texas Constitution, art. V, sec. 7](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm)).

  Duskie, of Killeen, is an Air Force veteran who had practiced law in Bell County for more than 20 years and was a partner and owner of Lindley, Wiley & Duskie PC in Killeen ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/three-republicans-vying-for-426th-district-court-judgeship/article_d9f35960-4f74-11ea-9617-2790fe7a8695.html)). He had been a partner at the firm since 2002, handled criminal defense, civil litigation and family law, and taught criminal law courses online for the University of Phoenix beginning in 2004; he was an associate editor of the St. Mary's Law Journal and interned at the federal district court in San Antonio in 1995 and at the Texas Supreme Court in 1996 ([Texas Lawyer](https://law.com/texaslawyer/2020/06/09/republican-lawyers-steve-duskie-and-jeff-parker-are-running-for-judge-of-texas-426th-district-court)). He holds a B.A. from Marymount College and a J.D. from St. Mary's University School of Law ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-gallagher-to-96th-judicial-district-court)). He also ran for the 264th District Court in 2018, finishing third with 18.5% of the vote ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/duskie-parker-to-vie-for-426th-judge-runoff/article_89f90810-3e0d-5b25-bd1a-5210e68d751d.html)).
