profile_summary: |
  Cari L. Starritt-Burnett is the judge of the 169th Judicial District Court in Bell County ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/169th_district_court/index.php)). Gov. Greg Abbott appointed her on Aug. 6, 2021, for a term set to expire Dec. 31, 2022 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-starritt-burnett-to-169th-judicial-district-court)). She succeeded Judge Gordon G. Adams, who retired in July 2021 after 21 years on the bench ([KWTX](https://www.kwtx.com/2021/08/06/governor-appoints-2-judges-fill-vacancies-central-texas-courts)). Adams administered her ceremonial oath and said she is the court's first woman judge ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/new-judge-sworn-in-starritt-burnett-replaces-adams-on-169th-court-bench/article_6053b78f-e867-542a-8433-e56b08c843f6.html)). In the November 2022 general election she was unopposed and declared elected ([Texas Secretary of State](https://newtools.cira.state.tx.us/upload/page/10782/docs/2022%20-%20NOVEMBER%208%20-%20GENERAL/BALLOT%20INFO/2022%20-%20NOVEMBER%208%20-%20STATEWIDE%20DISTRICT%20UNOPPOSED%20DECLARED%20ELECTED%20CERTIFICATION%20-%20REVISED.pdf)).

  Before her appointment she was a partner and co-owner of Seigman, Starritt-Burnett & Sinkfield, PLLC, where she headed the family law department ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-starritt-burnett-to-169th-judicial-district-court); [KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/new-judge-sworn-in-starritt-burnett-replaces-adams-on-169th-court-bench/article_6053b78f-e867-542a-8433-e56b08c843f6.html)). She holds a B.A. in government/political science from Angelo State University and a J.D. from Texas Tech University School of Law ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-starritt-burnett-to-169th-judicial-district-court)). District judges in Texas serve four-year terms ([Texas Constitution, art. V, sec. 7](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm)).

  **2026 election:** She was unopposed in the March 2026 Republican primary, and no Democrat filed ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/locals-vying-for-county-seats-in-primary-elections/article_6337d52a-83e9-5b75-8efe-8f6d35b2a54b.html)). She was the unopposed candidate for the 169th Judicial District on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Cari Starritt-Burnett was the only candidate for District Judge, 169th Judicial District on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot and received 70,347 votes in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
