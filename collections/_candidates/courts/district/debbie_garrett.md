Debbie Garrett is the presiding judge of the 27th Judicial District Court in Bell County, Texas. She was elected in November 2024 and took office on January 2, 2025. Judge Garrett previously served as a prosecutor in the Bell County District Attorney's Office. She is a graduate of Texas A&M University School of Law.
