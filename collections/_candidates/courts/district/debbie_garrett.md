profile_summary: |
  Debbie Garrett is the judge of the 27th Judicial District Court in Bell County ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/27th_district_court/index.php)). In the March 5, 2024 Republican primary for the open seat she led Cheyenne Minick with about 56% of the vote in election-night returns ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/voters-decide-on-2-bell-county-judges-while-jp-race-heads-to-runoff/article_bf4a1afd-18f2-5739-8519-fae866590517.html)), and she was the only candidate for the seat in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election, receiving 89,008 votes ([Bell County official results](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/bellcountytx/elections/Results/20241105_Summary_Results.pdf)). She took office Jan. 2, 2025, succeeding Judge John Gauntt, who retired Dec. 31, 2024 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)). District judges in Texas serve four-year terms ([Texas Constitution, art. V, sec. 7](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm)).

  Before her election she was an assistant district attorney in Bell County. She had worked as a prosecutor for 16 years handling misdemeanor and felony cases, including 11 years at the Gregg County Criminal District Attorney's Office before coming to Bell County ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/races-for-2-district-judges-jp-contested/article_fcc92fbd-549f-5159-907b-a94803067722.html)). During the 2024 campaign she named the court's docket backlog as its biggest challenge ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/voters-decide-on-2-bell-county-judges-while-jp-race-heads-to-runoff/article_bf4a1afd-18f2-5739-8519-fae866590517.html)). According to her campaign website, her earlier work also included serving as a drug court, grand jury and family-violence prosecutor, a family law and Child Protective Services mediator, a probation officer, and a clinical clerk for U.S. District Judge Sam Cummings ([campaign website](https://debbiegarrettforjudge.com/)).
