profile_summary: |
  John T. Gauntt was judge of the 27th District Court in Bell County from 2013 until his retirement on Dec. 31, 2024 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)). He was the 13th judge of the court, which was created in 1885 and hears criminal cases, and he succeeded Judge Joe Carroll, who had served 24 years; at his swearing-in he said he had spent some 40 years on the defense side of the courtroom ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/new-judges-spend-first-day-in-bell-county-courtrooms/article_07d7fc34-5629-11e2-bdd4-001a4bcf6878.html)). The 27th Judicial District covers Bell and Lampasas counties ([Texas Office of Court Administration](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1456598/district-judges-by-judicial-district.pdf)). As the 27th District Court's judge he presided over the Marvin Guy capital murder case, the county's oldest unresolved capital case at the time, and denied the state's request for a visiting judge ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/local-district-court-judge-will-continue-to-hear-marvin-guy-case-new-trial-date-to/article_3bb00bba-eaf6-11ed-90d2-7f16f171cf5e.html)).

  He did not seek re-election in 2024 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/races-for-2-district-judges-jp-contested/article_fcc92fbd-549f-5159-907b-a94803067722.html)). County officials honored him and retiring 146th District Judge Jack Jones at a December 2024 ceremony; his successor, Debbie Garrett, took office Jan. 2, 2025 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)). The Texas State Directory lists him as a Republican with a J.D. from Baylor University Law School ([Texas State Directory](https://txdirectory.com/online/person/?id=44632)).
