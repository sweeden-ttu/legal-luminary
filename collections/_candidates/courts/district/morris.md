profile_summary: |
  Rick Morris was appointed judge of the 146th Judicial District Court on May 5, 1989, and went on to serve six consecutive terms, hearing civil, family, and criminal cases; he also served as judge of the Bell County Drug Court from 2009 to 2015 ([Texas House, H.R. 1468 (2023)](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/88R/billtext/html/HR01468F.htm)). He retired in 2012 after more than 22 years on the bench ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Rick_Morris_(Texas))). Jack Jones was first elected to the 146th District Court seat in 2012, succeeding Morris ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html)).

  As of the May 2023 House resolution congratulating him on a Killeen ISD Distinguished Alumni Award, Morris was serving as a visiting judge and was of counsel to Baird, Crews, Schiller and Whitaker, P.C.; the resolution states he graduated from Killeen High School in 1969, earned a bachelor's degree from The University of Texas at Austin and a law degree from Baylor University School of Law, began in private practice and served as an assistant U.S. attorney, is a member of the State Bar of Texas, the Texas Center for the Judiciary and the Bell County Bar Association, is a life fellow of the Texas Bar Foundation, and was named Bell County Public Sector Lawyer of the Year in 2007 and 2012 ([Texas House, H.R. 1468 (2023)](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/88R/billtext/html/HR01468F.htm)). He was among those scheduled to administer oaths, as "Retired Judge Rick Morris," at the swearing-in of Bell County officials at the first Commissioners Court meeting of 2023 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-elected-officials-to-be-sworn-in-today/article_fb289b72-ffcf-56b6-a4cf-80b865153142.html)).

  Directory sources list him under his formal name: the Justia lawyer directory lists Judge Jack Richard Morris as Senior District Judge, 146th Judicial District Court, Baylor Law School J.D. ([Justia](https://lawyers.justia.com/lawyer/judge-jack-richard-morris-211228)), and trellis.law lists Jack R. Morris, 146th District Court of Texas, as retired ([trellis.law](https://trellis.law/judges/tx/bell)).
