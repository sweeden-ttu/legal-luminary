profile_summary: |
  Mike Russell is the judge of the 146th Judicial District Court in Bell County ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/district_courts/146th_district_court/index.php)). A Temple lawyer, he announced his candidacy for the open seat at an August 2023 campaign kickoff, describing a practice that covered civil matters including contracts, personal injury, trusts and estates, torts, real estate, employment and family law ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/races-for-2-district-judges-jp-contested/article_fcc92fbd-549f-5159-907b-a94803067722.html)). In the March 5, 2024 Republican primary he led Ashley Clapper Bennett with about 63% of the vote in election-night returns, and he said his Child Protective Services experience would apply to the CPS cases heard by the 146th ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/voters-decide-on-2-bell-county-judges-while-jp-race-heads-to-runoff/article_bf4a1afd-18f2-5739-8519-fae866590517.html)). He was the only candidate for the seat in the Nov. 5, 2024 general election, receiving 87,199 votes ([Bell County official results](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/bellcountytx/elections/Results/20241105_Summary_Results.pdf)).

  He took office Jan. 2, 2025, succeeding Judge Jack Jones, who retired Dec. 31, 2024 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)). He was among the county officials sworn in at the Bell County Justice Center at the start of 2025, with a Bell County Bar Association investiture set for Jan. 10 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-rings-in-new-year-by-swearing-in-elected-officials/article_878b3459-1a41-52ae-8110-9a069837be02.html)). District judges in Texas serve four-year terms ([Texas Constitution, art. V, sec. 7](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/cn/htm/cn.5.htm)).
