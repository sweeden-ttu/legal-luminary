profile_summary: |
  Joe Carroll Jr. (Nov. 20, 1943 – Nov. 17, 2025) was elected judge of the 27th Judicial District in 1988 and served about 24 years; he died at his home in Salado and was buried at the Texas State Cemetery ([Texas State Cemetery](https://cemetery.texas.gov/locate-a-plot/plotholder/joe-carroll)). The 1996 Texas judicial directory lists him as judge of the 27th District, serving Bell and Lampasas counties and residing in Belton ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/All_Archived_documents/JudicialInformation/pubs/ar96/distjud.pdf)).

  When John Gauntt was sworn in as judge of the 27th District Court, the Killeen Daily Herald reported that Gauntt succeeded Carroll, who "sat behind the bench for 24 years" ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/new-judges-spend-first-day-in-bell-county-courtrooms/article_07d7fc34-5629-11e2-bdd4-001a4bcf6878.html)).

  Before taking the bench he was appointed Bell County Attorney in 1970 and elected District Attorney for Bell, Lampasas and Mills counties in 1972; he later founded the Temple law firm Carroll, Carroll & Brown ([Dignity Memorial](https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/temple-tx/joe-carroll-12620396)).
