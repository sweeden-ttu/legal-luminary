profile_summary: |
  Jack W. Jones was judge of the 146th District Court in Bell County from January 2013 until his retirement on Dec. 31, 2024 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html); [KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)). In 2012 he won a three-candidate Republican primary field that included Mary Black Pearson and Brett Pritchard, then defeated Pearson in the runoff with 56% of the vote; he was unopposed in the November 2012 and 2016 general elections ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html)). He succeeded Judge Rick Morris and was the sixth judge of the court, which was created in 1960 and handles mainly civil and family law cases ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/new-judges-spend-first-day-in-bell-county-courtrooms/article_07d7fc34-5629-11e2-bdd4-001a4bcf6878.html)). He announced that he would seek a third term in the Republican primary held March 3, 2020 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html)), and the Office of Court Administration's May 2023 roster lists him as the 146th District Court's judge ([Texas Office of Court Administration](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1456598/district-judges-by-judicial-district.pdf)).

  He did not seek re-election in 2024 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/races-for-2-district-judges-jp-contested/article_fcc92fbd-549f-5159-907b-a94803067722.html)). At a December 2024 ceremony honoring his service he said he would take senior judge status, making him available as a visiting judge; his successor, Mike Russell, took office Jan. 2, 2025 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/retiring-bell-county-judges-jones-gauntt-recognized/article_933979a8-2023-5696-8ade-192f7af585a4.html)).

  Before taking the bench he practiced law for more than 30 years and served 14 years as a Temple College trustee ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html)); Ballotpedia lists him as a former partner at Jones and Harrell, PC ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Jack_Jones)). He holds undergraduate and law degrees from Baylor University ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/jones-seeks-third-term-as-146th-district-court-judge/article_b565c5cc-51f5-5489-8ae9-7d6d5436115e.html)).
