profile_summary: |
  G.W. Ivey served as Bell County Justice of the Peace, Precinct 3, Place 2. The Bell County Commissioners Court accepted his resignation at a Monday meeting; his last day in office was June 24, and he was leaving after nine consecutive terms, which the report put at nearly 36 years in the office ([Belton Journal](https://beltonjournal.com/bell-county-commissioners-appoint-wilkey-to-serve-as-justice-of-the-peace-pct-3-place-2)). He had not run for re-election in the March primary, and he said he would continue to serve as city judge for Rogers and Troy one day a month ([Belton Journal](https://beltonjournal.com/bell-county-commissioners-appoint-wilkey-to-serve-as-justice-of-the-peace-pct-3-place-2)).

  Commissioners appointed Temple Police Sgt. Larry Wilkey to fill the vacated seat and finish the unexpired term ending Jan. 1, 2023 ([Belton Journal](https://beltonjournal.com/bell-county-commissioners-appoint-wilkey-to-serve-as-justice-of-the-peace-pct-3-place-2)). Wilkey retired from the Temple Police Department in June 2022 and took office as justice of the peace the next day ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/wilkey-announces-2026-campaign-for-precinct-3-place-2-justice-of-the-peace/article_82996a98-dd06-5c4c-bc27-612093ee9a5c.html)).

  The trellis.law directory of Bell County judges lists G.W. Ivey, Bell County Justice of the Peace Court (Precinct 3, Place 2), as retired ([trellis.law](https://trellis.law/judges/tx/bell)).
