profile_summary: |
  Keith Reed, a former police chief in Troy and Lorena, was appointed by Bell County Judge David Blackburn as a temporary Justice of the Peace for Precinct 3, Place 1 after Judge David Barfield informed county officials that he could not serve because of a medical condition; county spokesman James Stafford said Reed had more than three and a half decades of law enforcement experience ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-commissioners-to-name-temporary-replacement-for-jp-barfield/article_66e91d22-7bea-5415-a46d-0930b72f54bc.html)). Reed, then 68, was sworn in by Blackburn at a special Commissioners Court meeting held under COVID-19 precautions; it was his first time serving as a justice of the peace, and he said he would take the required classes before starting and that he had worked as an adjunct professor with a focus on constitutional law and search and seizure ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/social-distanced-swearing-in-bell-county-officials-take-office-differently-due-to-covid-19/article_16a59644-4ee2-11eb-b86f-db46af296d42.html)).

  He was listed as Justice of the Peace, Precinct 3, Place 1, among the Bell County officials to be sworn in at the first Commissioners Court meeting of 2023 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-elected-officials-to-be-sworn-in-today/article_fb289b72-ffcf-56b6-a4cf-80b865153142.html)). The trellis.law directory of Bell County judges lists Keith Reed, Bell County Justice of the Peace Court (Precinct 3, Place 1), as retired ([trellis.law](https://trellis.law/judges/tx/bell)); Bell County's website now lists Rosanne Fisher as the judge of that court ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_3_place_1/index.php)).
