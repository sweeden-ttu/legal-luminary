profile_summary: |
  Rosanne Fisher is the Justice of the Peace for Bell County Precinct 3, Place 1; Bell County lists her court at the Bell County Temple Annex, 205 E. Central Avenue, Temple ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_3_place_1/index.php)). She was sworn in with other newly elected county officials in January 2025 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-rings-in-new-year-by-swearing-in-elected-officials/article_878b3459-1a41-52ae-8110-9a069837be02.html)).

  Fisher was one of three Republicans who ran in the March 2024 primary for the Precinct 3, Place 1 seat then held by Keith Reed. Before her candidacy she worked for two years as a veteran outreach employment transition coordinator for the Texas Military Department and My Career Path Staffing, and before that for more than six years as a freelance private investigator ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/bell-county-voters-to-decide-on-new-sheriff-judge-and-da/article_2e1122b0-cdc6-11ee-a292-4f44a95d2ade.html)).
