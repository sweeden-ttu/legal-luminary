profile_summary: |
  Don Engleking served as Bell County Justice of the Peace for Precinct 2. The Salado Village Voice reported that he took office on January 1, 2004, after the Bell County Commissioners Court appointed him to fill an unexpired term, and that he would not seek a fourth term, with his term ending December 31, 2018 ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/engleking-will-not-seek-fourth-term-jp)). The same report said he would continue as municipal judge for the Village of Salado and that his public service included the U.S. Air Force, the Amarillo Police Department and the Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/engleking-will-not-seek-fourth-term-jp)).

  In 2015 the Killeen Daily Herald reported that Bell County Justices of the Peace Don Engleking and Ted Duffield had ceased performing marriage ceremonies, identifying Engleking as overseeing the Precinct 2 Justice Court ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/two-bell-county-jps-not-performing-marriages/article_76fc0f4a-1f96-11e5-9802-8b35d0b816b8.html)). When Cliff Coleman announced in October 2017 that he would run for the Precinct 2 seat, he thanked Engleking for "twelve-plus years of honorable and dedicated service" as Precinct 2 Justice of the Peace ([Salado Village Voice](https://saladovillagevoice.com/coleman-will-run-justice-peace-precinct-2)).
