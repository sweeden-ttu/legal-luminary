profile_summary: |
  Beatrice "Bea" Cox was the Republican nominee for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 4, Place 2, which covers the Killeen area. She was unopposed in the March 3, 2026 Republican primary ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/jp-for-killeen-quarles-gonzalez-head-to-runoff-election-as-incumbent-finishes-3rd/article_baed7726-ac7b-42e9-bfc8-1a9515ac424c.html)) and faced Democrat Jessica A. Gonzalez, who won the May 26 Democratic runoff ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/gonzalez-a-killeen-councilwoman-wins-democratic-jp-primary-runoff/article_86fc56ce-ab7a-4d23-8bdc-d4b7b1a1bf86.html)). It is the only contested local judicial race on the Nov. 3, 2026 Bell County ballot ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)). The seat is held by Nicola J. James, who finished third in the Democratic primary ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/justice_of_the_peace/precinct_4_place_2/index.php); [KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/jp-for-killeen-quarles-gonzalez-head-to-runoff-election-as-incumbent-finishes-3rd/article_baed7726-ac7b-42e9-bfc8-1a9515ac424c.html)).

  **Background:** Cox is the Bell County truancy master, presiding over juvenile truancy cases for all school districts in the county. At an Oct. 7, 2026 candidate forum hosted by the Herald, she said she worked in the justice of the peace office for more than two decades, including many years under longtime JP Bill Cooke, now Bell County sheriff, and that she grew up in the Killeen area after her father served in the military ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/cox-and-gonzalez-participate-in-justice-of-the-peace-forum-ahead-of-nov-3-election/article_f3226aea-4314-4639-9f1d-804bfbe9c839.html)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Beatrice "Bea" Cox lost the race for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 4, Place 2 with 18,588 votes (37.1%); opponent: Jessica A. Gonzalez (D) 31,485 (62.9%), in unofficial returns ([Bell County Elections](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/); [Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/killeen-mayor-pro-tem-jessica-gonzalez-steamrolls-to-victory-in-bell-county-jp-race/article_a4e03829-e513-4185-a9c0-547fe8c4823e.html); [Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/here-s-a-rundown-of-area-election-results-from-tuesday-night/article_61b4fae6-2d44-4b71-a8c9-cca408c33344.html)).
