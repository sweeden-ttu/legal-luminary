profile_summary: |
  David Barfield served as Bell County Justice of the Peace for Precinct 3, Place 1, based in Temple ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-commissioners-to-name-temporary-replacement-for-jp-barfield/article_66e91d22-7bea-5415-a46d-0930b72f54bc.html)). In 2015 the Killeen Daily Herald, reporting on which Bell County justices of the peace would perform marriages after the U.S. Supreme Court's same-sex marriage ruling, named Judges David Barfield and G.W. Ivey among those who had not yet said whether they would ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/two-bell-county-jps-not-performing-marriages/article_76fc0f4a-1f96-11e5-9802-8b35d0b816b8.html)). Ahead of the 2020 elections the Herald listed "Precinct 3, Place 1, Justice of the Peace David Barfield" among the unopposed Bell County incumbents ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/bell-county-jp-sheriff-constable-seats-up-in-may-election/article_0f9ab026-2e8d-11ea-83f4-f3277cb4b5a3.html)).

  At the swearing-in of officials elected in November 2020, the Herald reported that Barfield had told county officials he could not serve because of a medical condition and was sworn in over the phone ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/social-distanced-swearing-in-bell-county-officials-take-office-differently-due-to-covid-19/article_16a59644-4ee2-11eb-b86f-db46af296d42.html)). County Judge David Blackburn then moved to appoint Keith Reed as a temporary replacement under the Texas Government Code until Barfield could return to duty ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-commissioners-to-name-temporary-replacement-for-jp-barfield/article_66e91d22-7bea-5415-a46d-0930b72f54bc.html)). The court-records aggregator Trellis lists Keith Reed as retired and Rosanne Fisher as the active judge of the Precinct 3, Place 1 justice court ([Trellis](https://trellis.law/judges/TX/bell)).
