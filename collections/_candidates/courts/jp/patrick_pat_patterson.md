profile_summary: |
  Patrick "Pat" Patterson was the Republican nominee for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 1 in Bell County on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot, where he was unopposed ([branch.vote](https://branch.vote/races/2026-texas-general-election-tx-state-justice-of-the-peace-tx-bell-justice-precinct-1)).

  In the March 3, 2026 Republican primary he defeated Joseph "Joey" Agee, 3,756 votes to 2,933 in the unofficial count; no Democrat filed for the seat, so the primary effectively decided the November winner. He is the stepson of Precinct 1 Justice of the Peace Theodore "Ted" Duffield, who has held the post since 1995 and whose term ends Jan. 1. The precinct serves the Belton area, Morgan's Point Resort, western Temple and northern Bell County ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/patterson-to-follow-in-stepfathers-justice-of-the-peace-footsteps-blackburn-keeps-county-judge-seat/article_95f7afcb-3ab6-5651-9b0b-d42467c264f5.html)).

  Ahead of the primary, the Herald reported that Patterson campaigned on three priorities: consistent application of the law, accessibility and transparency, and community trust and engagement ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/republican-primary-candidates-battle-for-bell-county-tx-justice-of-the-peace-precinct-1/article_cc04db82-7e73-5c9d-9988-7ca375e81338.html)). According to his campaign website, he was raised, went to school and works in the Precinct 1 community ([Patterson campaign website](https://patterson4jp1.com)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Patrick "Pat" Patterson was the only candidate for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 1 on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot and received 18,652 votes in unofficial returns ([Bell County Elections](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/)).
