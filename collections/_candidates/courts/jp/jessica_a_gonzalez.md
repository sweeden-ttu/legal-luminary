profile_summary: |
  Jessica A. Gonzalez is the Democratic nominee for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 4, Place 2, which covers much of Killeen and Harker Heights. She won the May 26, 2026 Democratic runoff over LaTasha Quarles, 1,233 votes (51.3%) to 1,171 (48.7%), in final unofficial results ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/gonzalez-a-killeen-councilwoman-wins-democratic-jp-primary-runoff/article_86fc56ce-ab7a-4d23-8bdc-d4b7b1a1bf86.html)). In the March 3, 2026 primary she finished second with 4,415 votes (33.44%) behind Quarles (5,110; 38.70%), while incumbent Nicola J. James finished third ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/jp-for-killeen-quarles-gonzalez-head-to-runoff-election-as-incumbent-finishes-3rd/article_baed7726-ac7b-42e9-bfc8-1a9515ac424c.html)). She faces Republican Beatrice "Bea" Cox, who was unopposed in her primary, on the Nov. 3 ballot ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/gonzalez-a-killeen-councilwoman-wins-democratic-jp-primary-runoff/article_86fc56ce-ab7a-4d23-8bdc-d4b7b1a1bf86.html)); it is the only contested local judicial race on the Bell County ballot ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Background:** Gonzalez has served on the Killeen City Council since 2021 ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/gonzalez-a-killeen-councilwoman-wins-democratic-jp-primary-runoff/article_86fc56ce-ab7a-4d23-8bdc-d4b7b1a1bf86.html)), representing District 1 in north Killeen ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/recall-petition-submitted-for-mayor-pro-tem-gonzalez-awaits-verification/article_86e51adc-4aca-11ef-adf1-a73104b940e4.html)), and is the city's mayor pro tem. At an Oct. 7, 2026 candidate forum hosted by the Herald, she said her work as a property manager, including eviction proceedings, has prepared her for the office, cited the mandatory 80-hour state training for new judges, and said she would step down from the council if elected ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/cox-and-gonzalez-participate-in-justice-of-the-peace-forum-ahead-of-nov-3-election/article_f3226aea-4314-4639-9f1d-804bfbe9c839.html)).

# Council record

<img src="/assets/images/candidates/jessica_a_gonzalez/photo2.jpg" alt="Jessica Gonzalez" class="flow-img-right" />In 2024, Killeen residents Janice Taylor and former councilwoman Mellisa Brown submitted a petition to recall Gonzalez from her District 1 council seat; the city charter required 246 valid signatures from District 1 voters ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/recall-petition-submitted-for-mayor-pro-tem-gonzalez-awaits-verification/article_86e51adc-4aca-11ef-adf1-a73104b940e4.html)). The city found the petition at least 33 signatures short after more than 60 of the 274 submitted were disqualified, so no recall went to the ballot ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/city-effort-to-oust-gonzalez-needed-dozens-of-more-signatures/article_1bf47288-4f8c-11ef-a366-87cf10d161f9.html); [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mayor_and_city_council_recall,_Killeen,_Texas_(2024-2025))).

<img src="/assets/images/candidates/jessica_a_gonzalez/photo4.jpg" alt="Jessica Gonzalez" class="flow-img-left" />After Killeen voters approved Proposition A, the marijuana-decriminalization ordinance, on Nov. 8, 2022 with 69% support ([KWTX](https://www.kwtx.com/2022/12/10/explainer-look-what-is-allowed-under-prop-killeen)), Gonzalez made the motion that removed Section 22-83, the provision barring police from using the odor of marijuana as probable cause for a search or seizure, and asked that the item return in three months; the council approved it 4–3. The rest of the ordinance, including its limits on citations and arrests for Class A and B marijuana possession and its ban on paraphernalia citations in place of a possession charge, remained in place ([KDH](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/killeen-city-council-removes-probable-cause-provision-from-proposition-a/article_9779723c-75e0-11ed-ae6c-43101dbd1598.html); [Fox 44](https://www.fox44news.com/news/local-news/bell-county/killeen-city-council-amends-marijuana-proposition/)).
