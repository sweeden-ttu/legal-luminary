profile_summary: |
  F.B. "Bob" McGregor Jr. is a senior judge of the 66th Judicial District Court, and is listed on the Third Administrative Judicial Region's 2024 roster of visiting judges ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1452218/3rd-ajr-visiting-judges.pdf)). The region covers 26 counties, including Bell, and its presiding judge may assign visiting judges to hold court in the region ([Texas Courts](https://txcourts.gov/3ajr/)).

  He began serving as judge of the 66th District Court in 1993, was re-elected without opposition in 2010, did not seek re-election, and left office at the end of his term on Dec. 31, 2014 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/F._B._McGregor,_Jr.)). The 1996 Texas judicial directory lists him as judge of the 66th District, serving Hill County and residing in Hillsboro ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/All_Archived_documents/JudicialInformation/pubs/ar96/distjud.pdf)).
