profile_summary: |
  Dib Waldrip of New Braunfels was appointed Presiding Judge of the Third Administrative Judicial Region by Governor Greg Abbott on Nov. 30, 2022, for a four-year term; he has served as judge of the 433rd Judicial District Court in Comal County since 2007 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-waldrip-presiding-judge-of-the-third-administrative-judicial-region)).

  The Third Administrative Judicial Region covers 26 counties, including Bell County, and its presiding judge has authority to assign visiting judges to hold court in the region ([Texas Courts](https://txcourts.gov/3ajr/)). Bell County is listed among the region's counties in Section 74.042(d), Government Code ([Texas Legislature Online](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/85R/billtext/html/SB01893F.htm)).

  The region's 2024 list of visiting judges includes Waldrip as an active judge of the 433rd Judicial District Court and presiding judge of the region ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1452218/3rd-ajr-visiting-judges.pdf)).

  He is a Republican; he was re-elected to the 433rd District Court without opposition in 2012 and ran unsuccessfully in the 2018 Republican primary for Place 8 on the Texas Court of Criminal Appeals ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Dib_Waldrip)).
