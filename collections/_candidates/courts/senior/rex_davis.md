profile_summary: |
  Rex Davis is listed as a senior appellate judge of the Tenth Court of Appeals on the Third Administrative Judicial Region's 2024 roster of visiting judges ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1452218/3rd-ajr-visiting-judges.pdf)). The region covers 26 counties, including Bell, and its presiding judge may assign visiting judges to hold court in the region ([Texas Courts](https://txcourts.gov/3ajr/)).

  The Tenth Court of Appeals lists Rex D. Davis as Chief Justice from May 31, 1996, to Aug. 4, 2003, and as Justice, Place 2, from Jan. 1, 2009, to Dec. 31, 2020 ([Texas Courts](https://txcourts.gov/10thcoa/about-the-court/history)).

  He is a Republican; he was unopposed in the March 4, 2014 Republican primary and the Nov. 4, 2014 general election for Place 2, did not file for re-election in 2020, and left office Dec. 31, 2020. He practiced as a trial lawyer in Central Texas from 1974 to 1996 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Rex_Davis_(Texas))).
