profile_summary: |
  James "Jimmy" Carroll is a senior judge of the Third Court of Appeals and is listed on the Third Administrative Judicial Region's 2024 roster of visiting judges ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1452218/3rd-ajr-visiting-judges.pdf)). The region covers 26 counties, including Bell, and its presiding judge may assign visiting judges to hold court in the region ([Texas Courts](https://txcourts.gov/3ajr/)).

  The 1995 Texas courts-of-appeals directory lists Jimmy L. Carroll as Chief Justice of the Third Court of Appeals in Austin, with a term expiring Dec. 31, 1996 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/All_Archived_documents/JudicialInformation/pubs/ar95/coas/COADIR95.pdf)). The Texas State Cemetery lists James L. "Jimmy" Carroll, born Jan. 22, 1948, as "Associate and Chief Justice, Court of Appeals, Third District of Texas" ([Texas State Cemetery](https://cemetery.texas.gov/locate-a-plot/plotholder/james-l-carroll)).
