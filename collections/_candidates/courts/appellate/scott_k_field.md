profile_summary: |
  Scott K. Field is a justice of the Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 2; Gov. Greg Abbott appointed him on June 11, 2024, and he assumed office on Sept. 1, 2024 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Field)). He was the first judge of the 480th District Court in Williamson County and earlier served a six-year term on the Third Court of Appeals ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/15thcoa/about-the-court/justices/justice-scott-field/); [San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 2. Incumbent; appointed by Gov. Greg Abbott ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Field)). First election for the court ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)). Opponent on the ballot: Tom Baker (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Scott K. Field lost the race for Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 2 with 4,618,116 votes (48.0%); opponent: Tom Baker (D) 4,998,619 (52.0%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
