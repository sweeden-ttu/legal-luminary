  Thomas Smith worked for Attorney General Ken Paxton in the Attorney General's office and in Paxton's Texas Senate office ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/); [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/09/29/texas-court-of-criminal-appeals-2026-election/)). He won the May 26, 2026 Republican primary runoff over Alison Fox ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Thomas_Smith_%28Texas%29)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Texas Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 3. Open seat; Judge Bert Richardson is not seeking re-election. Opponent on the ballot: Okey Anyiam (D), Mark Ash (LIB) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Thomas Smith lost the race for Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 3 with 4,601,878 votes (47.6%); opponents: Okey Anyiam (D) 4,839,230 (50.0%), Mark Ash (LIB) 231,048 (2.4%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
