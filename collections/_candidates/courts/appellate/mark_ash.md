  Mark Ash is a criminal defense attorney with a law degree from South Texas College of Law ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)).   **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Texas Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 3. Open seat. Opponent on the ballot: Thomas Smith (R), Okey Anyiam (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Mark Ash lost the race for Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 3 with 231,048 votes (2.4%); opponents: Thomas Smith (R) 4,601,878 (47.6%), Okey Anyiam (D) 4,839,230 (50.0%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
