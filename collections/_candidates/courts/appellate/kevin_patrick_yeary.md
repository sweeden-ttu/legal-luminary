  Kevin Patrick Yeary was first elected to the Court of Criminal Appeals in 2014 and is the only incumbent on that court seeking re-election this year ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/); [Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/cca/about-the-court/judges/)). He earned his law degree from St. Mary's University and was a prosecutor in Dallas, Houston and San Antonio ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Texas Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 4. Incumbent; first elected in 2014. Opponent on the ballot: Audra Riley (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Kevin Patrick Yeary lost the race for Court of Criminal Appeals, Place 4 with 4,659,299 votes (48.3%); opponent: Audra Riley (D) 4,996,898 (51.7%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
