  Cory Liu is judge of the 250th District Court in Travis County; Gov. Greg Abbott announced his appointment on March 5, 2025, for a term that expires Dec. 31, 2026 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-liu-to-250th-judicial-district-court)). He holds a bachelor's degree in political science from the University of Chicago and a law degree from Harvard Law School, and was an attorney at Butler Snow LLP in Austin before his appointment ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-liu-to-250th-judicial-district-court)). He was unopposed in the March 3, 2026 Republican primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Cory_Liu)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Cory Liu lost the race for Third Court of Appeals, Chief Justice with 518,024 votes (40.6%); opponent: Darlene Byrne (D) 758,920 (59.4%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/vote-2026/2026/10/30/judicial-election-results-in-texas-and-san-antonio-area-on-nov-3-2026/)).
