  Cory Liu is judge of the 250th District Court in Travis County; Gov. Greg Abbott announced his appointment on March 5, 2025, for a term that expires Dec. 31, 2026 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-liu-to-250th-judicial-district-court)). He was unopposed in the March 3, 2026 Republican primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Cory_Liu)).
