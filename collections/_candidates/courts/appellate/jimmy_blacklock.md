profile_summary: |
  Jimmy Blacklock has served on the Texas Supreme Court since 2018 and was previously general counsel to Gov. Greg Abbott ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)). He was appointed chief justice by Gov. Abbott in January 2025 ([Office of the Governor](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-appoints-blacklock-as-chief-justice-sullivan-as-justice-of-the-supreme-court-of-texas); [Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/supreme/about-the-court/justices.aspx)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Texas Supreme Court, Chief Justice. Incumbent chief justice. Opponent on the ballot: Maggie Ellis (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Jimmy Blacklock lost the race for Texas Supreme Court, Chief Justice with 4,625,886 votes (47.6%); opponent: Maggie Ellis (D) 5,102,288 (52.4%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
