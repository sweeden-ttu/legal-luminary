profile_summary: |
  Scott Brister is chief justice of the Fifteenth Court of Appeals; Gov. Greg Abbott appointed him on June 11, 2024, and he assumed office on Sept. 1, 2024 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Brister)). The court was created in the 2023 legislative session, and its three inaugural justices are on the ballot for the first time in 2026 ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)). He holds a law degree from Harvard Law School and previously served on the Texas Supreme Court from 2003 to 2009 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/15thcoa/about-the-court/justices/chief-justice-scott-brister/)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Chief Justice. Incumbent; appointed by Gov. Greg Abbott ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Scott_Brister)). First election for the court ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)). Also running: Jerry Zimmerer (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).
