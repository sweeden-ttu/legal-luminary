profile_summary: |
  Darlene Byrne has been chief justice of the Third Court of Appeals since Jan. 1, 2021, after defeating incumbent Jeff L. Rose in November 2020 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Darlene_Byrne)). Before that she served 20 years as presiding judge of the 126th District Court in Travis County, where she founded the Travis County Family Drug Treatment Court ([Third Court of Appeals](https://txcourts.gov/3rdcoa/about-the-court/justices/chief-justice-darlene-byrne/)). She was unopposed in the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Darlene_Byrne)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Third Court of Appeals, Chief Justice. Incumbent since Jan. 1, 2021 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Darlene_Byrne)). Opponent on the ballot: Cory Liu (R) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** Darlene Byrne won the race for Third Court of Appeals, Chief Justice with 758,920 votes (59.4%); opponent: Cory Liu (R) 518,024 (40.6%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/vote-2026/2026/10/30/judicial-election-results-in-texas-and-san-antonio-area-on-nov-3-2026/)).
