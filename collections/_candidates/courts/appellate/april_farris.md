  April Farris is a justice of the Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 3; Gov. Greg Abbott appointed her on June 11, 2024, and she assumed office on Sept. 1, 2024 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/April_Farris)). She previously served on the First Court of Appeals in Houston, where she was appointed in 2021 and elected in 2022 ([Texas Courts](https://www.txcourts.gov/15thcoa/about-the-court/justices/justice-april-farris/); [San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)).


  **On the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot:** Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 3. Incumbent; appointed by Gov. Greg Abbott ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/April_Farris)). First election for the court ([San Antonio Report](https://sanantonioreport.org/voter-guide-meet-the-judicial-candidates-on-the-nov-3-ballot/)). Opponent on the ballot: Marc M. Meyer (D) ([Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)).

  **Nov. 3, 2026 result:** April Farris lost the race for Fifteenth Court of Appeals, Place 3 with 4,654,820 votes (48.5%); opponent: Marc M. Meyer (D) 4,950,446 (51.5%), in unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html); [The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)).
