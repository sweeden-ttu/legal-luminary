Pete Flores, a Republican from Pleasanton, represents District 24 in the Texas Senate, where he chairs the Criminal Justice Committee; he served 27 years as a Texas game warden, first won a Senate seat in a 2018 special election, and has held District 24 since 2023 ([Texas Senate](https://senate.texas.gov/member.php?d=24)). District 24 stretches from the Killeen area to Atascosa County, and Killeen is its largest city ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/killeen-s-state-senator-is-running-for-reelection/article_d1af6c00-0de8-4370-b1d5-b444ca377cd5.html)). He faced Democrat Joe P. Herrera in the November 3, 2026 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_State_Senate_District_24)) and won districtwide, 192,054 votes to 147,026, according to unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
