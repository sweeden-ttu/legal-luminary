Greg Abbott, a Republican, has been Governor of Texas since January 20, 2015; he previously served as Texas Attorney General (2002–2015) and on the Texas Supreme Court ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Greg_Abbott)). He won the March 3, 2026 Republican primary with 81.8% and faced Democrat Gina Hinojosa and Libertarian Pat Dixon in the November 3, 2026 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_gubernatorial_election,_2026)). Hinojosa won, 5,230,789 votes to Abbott's 4,524,527, according to unofficial statewide returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
