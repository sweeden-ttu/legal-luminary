Gina Hinojosa is the Democratic candidate for Governor of Texas in the November 3, 2026 general election, facing incumbent Republican Greg Abbott and Libertarian Pat Dixon ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_gubernatorial_election,_2026)). She has represented District 49 in the Texas House since 2017 and is not seeking re-election to that seat; she won the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary for governor with 58.5% ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Gina_Hinojosa)).
