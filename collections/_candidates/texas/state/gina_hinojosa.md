Gina Hinojosa was the Democratic candidate for Governor of Texas in the November 3, 2026 general election, against incumbent Republican Greg Abbott and Libertarian Pat Dixon ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Texas_gubernatorial_election,_2026)). She has represented District 49 in the Texas House since 2017 and did not seek re-election to that seat; she won the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary for governor with 58.5% ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Gina_Hinojosa)). She won the general election with 5,230,789 votes to Abbott's 4,524,527, according to unofficial statewide returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
