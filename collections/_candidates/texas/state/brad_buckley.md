Brad Buckley represents District 54, which covers part of Bell County, in the Texas House of Representatives ([Texas House](https://house.texas.gov/members/member-page/?district=54)). A Republican and a veterinarian, he took office on January 8, 2019 and chaired the House Public Education Committee in 2023–2024; he was unopposed in the March 3, 2026 Republican primary and faced Democrat Dawn Richardson in the November 3, 2026 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Brad_Buckley)), which he won 26,146 votes to 22,372, according to unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
