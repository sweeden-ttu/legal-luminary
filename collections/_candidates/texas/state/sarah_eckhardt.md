Sarah Eckhardt was the Democratic candidate for Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts in the November 3, 2026 general election, against Republican Don Huffines, Green Party candidate Shehla Faizi and Libertarian V. Alonzo Echavarria-Garza; she won the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary with 64.1%. She has represented District 14 in the Texas Senate since July 31, 2020 and previously served as Travis County Judge until resigning in May 2020 to run for the Senate ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Sarah_Eckhardt)). She won the comptroller's race with 5,014,496 votes to Huffines's 4,511,185, according to unofficial statewide returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
