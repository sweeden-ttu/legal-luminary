John Carter, a Republican, has represented Texas's 31st congressional district in the U.S. House since January 3, 2003; before Congress he was judge of the 277th District Court in Williamson County from 1981 to 2001 ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/John_Carter_(Texas))). He won the March 3, 2026 Republican primary with 59.7% and faced Democrat Justin Early and Green Party nominee Greg Stoker in the November 3, 2026 general election ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/John_Carter_(Texas)); [Bell County Elections ballot samples](https://www.bellcountytx.com/departments/elections/ballot_samples.php)). He won re-election districtwide with 164,012 votes to Early's 136,734, according to unofficial returns ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).
