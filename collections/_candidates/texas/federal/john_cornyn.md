John Cornyn, a Republican, has been a United States senator from Texas since December 2, 2002; his current term ends January 3, 2027. He previously served as Texas Attorney General (1999–2002) and on the Texas Supreme Court (1990–1997). He lost the May 26, 2026 Republican primary runoff to Ken Paxton, 63.8% to 36.2%, and is not on the November 3, 2026 ballot, where the Senate race is between Paxton (R) and James Talarico (D) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/John_Cornyn)).
