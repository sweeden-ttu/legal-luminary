Dan Yancey is the Mayor of Copperas Cove, Texas; the city lists his current term of office as November 2024 to November 2027 ([City of Copperas Cove](https://www.copperascovetx.gov/directory.aspx?EID=11)). Copperas Cove's mayor and seven council members are elected at large to three-year terms ([City of Copperas Cove](https://www.copperascovetx.gov/27/Government)).
