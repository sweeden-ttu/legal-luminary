Michael Blomquist is the Mayor of Harker Heights, Texas; the city lists his second term as expiring in May 2029 ([City of Harker Heights](https://www.harkerheights.gov/165/Mayor-City-Council)). He won a second three-year term in a June 13, 2026 runoff over Lynda Nash, 1,251 votes to 1,119, after leading the May 2, 2026 election with 42.7% ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/harker_heights_herald/blomquist-sworn-in-as-mayor-again-council-elects-halabi-mayor-pro-tem/article_1cd1e891-dabf-4b08-9143-c9536fe88d70.html)).
