Riakos Adams, a former Army officer who served three terms on the Killeen City Council, was appointed mayor by the council, while serving as mayor pro tem, to fill the vacancy created when Mayor Debbie Nash-King resigned to run for an at-large council seat ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/adams-becomes-new-mayor-of-killeen/article_0fa770d8-b6a9-45b9-aa64-ceb7b8124dc8.html)). He ran for a full term in the May 2, 2026 election and finished third behind Joseph Solomon and Jose Segarra ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/newly-elected-killeen-council-and-mayor-to-swear-in-tuesday/article_4b04f44c-9f39-4d68-8647-874a4b63725d.html)). The city now lists Joseph Solomon as mayor, with a term running May 2026 to November 2027 ([City of Killeen](https://www.killeentexas.gov/153/City-Council)).
