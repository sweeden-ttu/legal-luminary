Tim Davis is the Mayor of Temple, Texas. He was first elected mayor in May 2018 after serving on the City Council for District 1 beginning in May 2013; he owns a Temple employee-benefits insurance agency and holds an accounting degree from Abilene Christian University ([City of Temple](https://www.templetx.gov/departments/city_council/meet_the_council.php)).
