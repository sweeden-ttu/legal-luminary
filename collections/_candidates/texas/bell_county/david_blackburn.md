David Blackburn is the County Judge of Bell County, Texas, and presides over the Bell County Commissioners Court ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/commissioners_court/index.php)). A Republican from Temple, he took office in 2019; he was Temple's city manager from 2005 until 2014 and announced a run for a third term ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/judge-david-blackburn-seeking-3rd-term-in-bell-county-seat/article_c9e8d0bd-a1f7-5f62-ad0f-5c315c67ee2d.html)). He won the March 3, 2026 Republican primary over Precinct 1 Justice of the Peace Ted Duffield, 65.66% to 34.34% ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-election-results-at-a-glance/article_7889bdf5-e780-52df-8904-8146148e10cd.html)); no Democrat filed, so the primary winner was unopposed in the November 3, 2026 general election ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/county-judge-candidates-vie-for-uncontested-position-in-general-election/article_cff38b6e-550a-5706-bafa-74e2a87d09ff.html)). He was elected without opposition on Nov. 3, receiving 71,105 votes in unofficial returns ([Bell County Elections](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/)).
