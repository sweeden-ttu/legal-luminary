Louie Minor is the Bell County Commissioner for Precinct 4 ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/commissioners_court/index.php)). A Democrat, he ran for re-election in the March 3, 2026 Democratic primary ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Louie_Minor_(Bell_County_Commissioner_Precinct_4,_Texas,_candidate_2026))) and lost to Ernest Wilkerson, 64.6% to 35.3% ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-election-results-at-a-glance/article_7889bdf5-e780-52df-8904-8146148e10cd.html)). He is not on the November 3, 2026 ballot; the Precinct 4 general election is between Wilkerson (D) and Curtis Emmons (R) ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Curtis_Emmons_(Bell_County_Commissioner_Precinct_4,_Texas,_candidate_2026))).
