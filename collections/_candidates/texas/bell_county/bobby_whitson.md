Bobby Whitson is the Bell County Commissioner for Precinct 2 ([Bell County](https://www.bellcountytx.com/county_government/commissioners_court/index.php)). A Republican from Salado, he took office in 2019 and announced in November 2025 that he would seek a third term ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-commissioner-bobby-whitson-seeks-3rd-term-in-2026-election/article_ede7bb7f-7d56-5c89-9780-e56f3b0e91fb.html)). He was the Republican candidate for Precinct 2 on the November 3, 2026 ballot, against Democrat Stacey L. Wilson ([Bell County Elections ballot samples](https://www.bellcountytx.com/departments/elections/ballot_samples.php); [Nov. 3 ballot guide](/nov-3-2026-ballot-guide/)). Wilson won, 15,958 votes (53.8%) to Whitson's 13,724 (46.2%), according to unofficial returns ([Bell County Elections](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/); [Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/region/bell-county-voters-deny-three-property-tax-measures-two-gop-seats-flip-one-race-headed/article_92fa86ea-909f-53ca-acc9-17a2a7bb64ee.html)).
