## Observations

- **Top 3 worst offenders** (Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Ocwen) account for **386,190 complaints** — 63% of all servicer complaints tracked.
- **Law firms rarely appear** in the CFPB database — only Zwicker & Associates and McCalla Raymer have entries, and those are primarily for debt collection, not mortgage servicing.
- **NewRez LLC** has zero CFPB footprint — it was formed in 2018 from the merger of New Penn Financial and Caliber. Its predecessor (Caliber) has 4,742 complaints.
- **Mr. Cooper vs Nationstar**: Combined total of 34,726, but these are partially overlapping — CFPB lists both names separately.
- **Shellpoint, LoanCare, ServiceMac** all appear in the database under their corporate parent names (Shellpoint Partners, LLC; LoanCare, LLC; SERVICEMAC, LLC).
