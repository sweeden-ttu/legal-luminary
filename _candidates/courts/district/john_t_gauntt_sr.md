John T. Gauntt Sr. served as judge of the 27th Judicial District Court in Bell County, Texas. He retired in December 2024 after serving on the bench and was succeeded by Judge Debbie Garrett. He is the father of John Gauntt Jr., candidate for County Court at Law No. 3.
