John Gauntt Jr. is the Republican nominee for Bell County Court at Law No. 3. A fourth-generation Bell County lawyer, he has served as a prosecutor in the Bell County Attorney's Office for 24 years, with extensive experience in juvenile law. He has served on the State Bar's Juvenile Law Section board for six years and as an officer for two years. Gauntt has trained police officers, probation officers, and attorneys across Texas on juvenile law. The County Court at Law No. 3 serves as the juvenile court for Bell County and also handles adult misdemeanor cases and specialty courts. The current judge is Rebecca DePew, who is not seeking re-election.
