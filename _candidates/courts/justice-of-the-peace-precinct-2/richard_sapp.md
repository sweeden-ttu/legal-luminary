Richard Sapp is the Republican nominee for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 2 in Bell County, Texas. A retired Temple Police Department detective with over 32 years of service, Sapp brings advanced and specialized training from the FBI, ATF, U.S. Marshals Service, Secret Service, and Texas Department of Public Safety. He has also served as an adjunct professor instructing criminal justice and law enforcement classes for over 20 years. The incumbent, Cliff Coleman, announced his retirement, and Sapp won a competitive four-way Republican primary. He faces no Democratic opposition in the November general election.
