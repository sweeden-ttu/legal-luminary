Paul A. Motz is the presiding judge of County Court at Law No. 1 in Bell County, Texas. Judge Motz also serves as the presiding judge for Civil Court, Probate Court, and Mental Health Court. The court is located at the Bell County Justice Center in Belton, Texas.
