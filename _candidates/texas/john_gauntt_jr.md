profile_summary: |
  John Gauntt Jr. is a fourth-generation Bell County attorney with a distinguished 25-year career as a prosecutor. He is currently seeking the bench of Bell County Court at Law No. 3, continuing a family legacy of legal service that spans over a century.

  ### Academic Background
  Continuing his family's academic tradition, John Gauntt Jr. earned his Juris Doctor from the **Baylor University School of Law**. His legal education is complemented by deep ties to the **University of Texas**, fostering a sophisticated understanding of Texas law and history.

  ### Professional History
  For nearly a quarter-century, Gauntt has served the residents of Bell County as a prosecutor in the County Attorney's Office. He is widely recognized as a specialist in juvenile law, having served on the Board of the State Bar of Texas Juvenile Law Section for over six years. His professional contributions include training law enforcement and legal colleagues statewide on juvenile justice issues.

  Gauntt's platform focuses on the fair and efficient administration of justice, with a particular emphasis on juvenile and family matters, drawing on his extensive experience in the "people's courts."
