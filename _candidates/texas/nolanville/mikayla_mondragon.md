# Candidate Profile Summary

Mikayla Mondragon was a nonpartisan candidate for Nolanville City Council Seat 4 in Texas. She was on the ballot for the general election held on November 4, 2025, where she ran against Michael Whitson. According to Ballotpedia, she did not complete their 2025 Candidate Connection survey, and no endorsements were identified for her in this election.

# Biographical Background

## Personal Summary

Mikayla Mondragon is a Native Texan and a mother of three children.

## Military Service

She is a disabled United States Navy Veteran. She served as a Heavy Equipment Operator in the Navy Seabees and was part of the NCBC 11 recommission in 2007.

## Education

Mikayla Mondragon graduated from Copperas Cove High School in 2003.

## Residency History

She first moved to Central Texas in 2000. After her military service, she returned to the Central Texas area in 2011 and has been a resident of Nolanville since 2014.
