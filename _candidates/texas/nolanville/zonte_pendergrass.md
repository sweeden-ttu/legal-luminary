# Executive Summary

Zonte Pendergrass ran for Nolanville City Council, Seat 2, in the general election on November 4, 2025, against opponents Juanita (Nita) Sims and Lawrence Reis. Professionally, he works in the defense and space sector for KBR and has a background in U.S. Army logistics and sustainment. Public records indicate a potential controversy; a third-party arrest aggregation site shows a Bell County booking record for Pendergrass dated October 20, 2023, related to an arrest by the Nolanville Police Department, although this is presented as an allegation without confirmed court disposition. While Ballotpedia did not post the final election outcome, local reporting indicated that the incumbent council members were on track to retain their seats.
