# Executive Summary

Juanita Sims is a councilmember for the City of Nolanville, Texas, holding Seat 2. She won the seat in the November 2023 election with 143 votes in a close race. As a councilmember, she has been active in city governance, participating in meetings and voting on key issues such as the appointment of an interim City Manager in April 2024. She was also a candidate for the same seat in the general election on November 4, 2025. Despite her public role, there is limited publicly available information regarding her specific campaign priorities, platform, or personal background. Candidate directories like Ballotpedia and Good Party do not list specific issue positions, and no formal endorsements were identified for her 2025 candidacy. Similarly, no campaign finance reports were found in statewide directories, and no controversies have been reported.
