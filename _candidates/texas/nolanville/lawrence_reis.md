  Lawrence Reis was a candidate for Seat 2 on the Nolanville City Council in the general election held on November 4, 2025. A retired veteran who has resided in the Nolanville area for approximately 30 years, Reis campaigned on a platform focused on upgrading the city's infrastructure and attracting new retail businesses, referencing past community discussions about bringing in major retailers like Walmart and H-E-B. He faced incumbent Juanita Sims and another challenger, Zonte Pendergrass. No official endorsements were found in the available public information. While official election results were not located, a local media report suggests that the incumbent, Juanita Sims, retained her seat, indicating Reis was not elected.
