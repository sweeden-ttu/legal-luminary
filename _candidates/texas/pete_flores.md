# Pete Flores

## Office
Texas Senate District 24

## Biography
Pete Flores is a Republican member of the Texas Senate representing District 24. He was first elected in a special election in 2018, becoming the first Hispanic Republican in the Texas Senate. He was re-elected in 2022 to serve Senate District 24. During the 88th Regular Legislative Session, he served as Vice Chair for the Senate Committees on Border Security and Criminal Justice. Senator Flores was named Chairman of the Senate Committee on Criminal Justice in January 2024. He also serves on the Finance, Education, and Water, Agriculture, and Rural Affairs committees. He lives in Pleasanton with his wife, Elizabeth.
