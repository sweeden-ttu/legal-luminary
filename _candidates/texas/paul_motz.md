profile_summary: |
  Judge Paul Abel Motz is the presiding judge of Bell County Court at Law No. 1. Elected in 2022, Judge Motz has established a reputation for professional excellence and accessibility, providing judicial services in both English and Spanish to the residents of Bell County.

  ### Academic Background
  Judge Motz earned his Doctor of Jurisprudence from the **Texas Southern University Thurgood Marshall School of Law** in 2001. His academic training provided a rigorous foundation in both civil and criminal jurisprudence.

  ### Professional History
  Before his election to the bench, Judge Motz maintained a diverse private practice for nearly two decades. His legal expertise spanned appellate law, probate, and family law, as well as criminal defense. He is admitted to practice in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Texas and has been a licensed member of the State Bar of Texas since 2005.

  In his current role, he handles a broad docket including Class A and B misdemeanors, civil litigation, and probate matters, bringing a balanced and experienced perspective to the court.
