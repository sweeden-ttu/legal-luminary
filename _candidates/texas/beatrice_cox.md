profile_summary: |
  Beatrice "Bea" Cox is a lifelong resident of Killeen with a career spanning 35 years of dedicated service within the Bell County judicial and law enforcement systems. Her campaign for Justice of the Peace is built on a foundation of deep operational knowledge and a commitment to the residents of Precinct 4.

  ### Professional History
  Cox’s professional path began in the Killeen Police Department before transitioning to a 23-year tenure within the Bell County Justice of the Peace system. Serving as Chief Clerk for long-time Justice of the Peace Bill Cooke, she gained unparalleled expertise in the daily management of Class C misdemeanors, small claims, and evictions.

  Notably, she was appointed as the first Truancy Master for Bell County, where she developed specialized programs to support local students and families. Her career is defined by her "hands-on" experience and her reputation for integrity and work ethic within the "people's court."

  ### Community Roots
  The daughter of a U.S. Army veteran, Cox is deeply rooted in the Killeen community. She is an active member of the Central Texas Republican Women-PAC and the LULAC Herencia Chapter, reflecting her broad engagement with the diverse residents of Central Texas.
