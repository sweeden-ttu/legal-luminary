  Jackie Ivicic is an incumbent and nonpartisan candidate running for re-election to the Bartlett City Council At-large (Place 2) in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. She currently holds the City Council - Place 2 seat, with a term from 2024 to 2026. Professionally, Ms. Ivicic serves as the Transportation and Maintenance Director for the Bartlett Independent School District (ISD). She has an extensive background in school transportation spanning over 20 years, having previously served as the Transportation Director for Jarrell ISD for a decade and as the President of the Heart of Texas chapter of the Texas Association for Pupil Transportation (TAPT).
