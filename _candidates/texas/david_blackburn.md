profile_summary: |
  Judge David Blackburn has served as the Bell County Judge since 2019, bringing nearly three decades of experience in municipal governance and legal service to the Commissioners Court. A native of Abilene, Texas, Judge Blackburn's career is marked by a deep commitment to the administrative and judicial health of Central Texas.

  ### Academic Background
  Judge Blackburn earned his Juris Doctorate from **St. Mary’s University School of Law** in 1985. Prior to his legal studies, he attended **Hardin-Simmons University**, where he graduated *cum laude* with a Bachelor of Arts in History and Political Science. His academic foundation also includes studies in International Affairs at **American University** in Washington, D.C.

  ### Professional History
  Before ascending to the bench, Judge Blackburn served as the City Manager for both Temple and Killeen, Texas. His extensive experience in public administration includes roles as City Attorney for Texarkana and Plainview. Since becoming County Judge, his tenure has focused on fiscal responsibility, infrastructure expansion, and the development of the county's first mental health diversion center.

  He is a long-standing member of the State Bar of Texas and has served as President of the Texas City Attorney’s Association.
