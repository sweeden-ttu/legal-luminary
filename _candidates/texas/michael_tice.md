  Tice's background is rooted in over two decades of law enforcement service. His experience in the field is complemented by his military service, where he developed a strong foundation in leadership and organizational management.

  ### Platform
  His campaign for Justice of the Peace focuses on the principles of "accountability, fairness, and transparency." He aims to leverage his extensive experience in public safety to ensure that the residents of Precinct 2 have access to an efficient and impartial judicial process.
