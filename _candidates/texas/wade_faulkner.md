profile_summary: |
  Judge Wade Faulkner is the presiding judge of the 478th District Court, a bench he has led since its creation in 2022. A retired Lieutenant Colonel in the U.S. Army, Judge Faulkner brings a wealth of experience in both military and civilian justice to the Bell County judiciary.

  ### Academic Background
  Judge Faulkner's academic credentials include a Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Military Law from **The Judge Advocate General's Legal Center and School** (2008). He earned his Juris Doctor from **Baylor Law School** in 2001 and his Bachelor of Arts in History, *cum laude*, from **Texas Tech University** in 1995.

  ### Professional History
  During his 23-year military career, Judge Faulkner served as a prosecutor, defense attorney, and military judge. Notably, he oversaw the high-profile court-martial prosecution related to the 2009 Fort Hood shooting. In civilian practice, he operated a private firm specializing in military justice and criminal law.

  As a judge, he presides over the Bell County Veteran’s Treatment Court, demonstrating a sophisticated commitment to rehabilitative justice for those who have served. He is a past president of the Bell County Bar Association.
