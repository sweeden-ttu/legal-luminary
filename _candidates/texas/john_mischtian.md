  Judge John Michael Mischtian has served as the presiding judge of Bell County Court at Law No. 2 for over a decade. His long-standing service is characterized by a deep familiarity with the local legal landscape and a commitment to the efficient administration of justice.

  ### Academic Background
  A double graduate of **Baylor University**, Judge Mischtian earned both his undergraduate degree and his Juris Doctor from the **Baylor University School of Law** (1993). His education at one of Texas’s premier legal institutions remains a cornerstone of his judicial philosophy.

  ### Professional History
  Licensed to practice in 1993, Judge Mischtian spent 17 years in private practice before taking the bench. His professional experience covered a wide spectrum of the law, including juvenile justice, probate, and criminal law. Since 2010, he has presided over Court at Law No. 2, managing cases ranging from misdemeanors to significant civil disputes.

  His tenure is marked by consistent re-elections, reflecting the community's trust in his experience and judicial temperament.
