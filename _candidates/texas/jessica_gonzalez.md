  Jessica A. Gonzalez is a distinguished public servant currently serving her third term on the Killeen City Council, where she represents District 1. A native of Central Texas—born in Temple and raised in Killeen—she has dedicated her career to the civic and economic advancement of the region.

  ### Academic and Professional Background
  Councilwoman Gonzalez's tenure on the city council is defined by her advocacy for transparency and her strategic focus on public safety and economic development. Her experience in municipal governance has provided her with a unique perspective on the intersection of local policy and the judicial system.

  In 2026, she transitioned her focus toward the judiciary, seeking the office of Justice of the Peace for Precinct 4, Place 2. Her platform emphasizes a "balanced and professional" administration of the bench, focusing on the efficient resolution of local matters within the "people's court." Following a successful primary and a decisive runoff victory, she stands as the Democratic nominee for the general election.

  ### Community Engagement
  Beyond her legislative duties, Gonzalez is deeply involved in local initiatives that support community growth and transparency. Her candidacy for the bench is a natural extension of her commitment to ensuring that the legal system is accessible and fair for all residents of Bell County.
