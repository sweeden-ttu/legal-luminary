# Executive Summary

John “Crow” Miller, age 60, is a candidate for one of the three Killeen City Council at-large seats in the May 2, 2026 election. He is one of 15 candidates in a crowded field. Miller's platform is centered on leveraging the arts as a tool for economic and community development, aiming to increase the city's prestige and add new revenue sources. His other stated priorities include addressing the city's 'growing pains' and implementing measures to keep jobs and spending within Killeen's city limits. As of late April 2026, no public endorsements or controversies have been reported in major local media coverage.

# Campaign Platform And Priorities

## Priority Area

Arts and Economic Development

## Description

John “Crow” Miller is 'banking on the city of Killeen’s interest in expanding arts.' He proposes using 'untapped funds' to help promote art within the city. This focus on the arts is part of a broader platform that also includes addressing the city's 'growing pains' and strengthening the local economy by ensuring jobs and consumer spending ('dollars') remain within Killeen's city limits.

## Stated Goal

To increase the prestige of the city, add more revenue sources to the city’s coffers, deal with urban growth challenges, and keep both jobs and dollars within Killeen.
