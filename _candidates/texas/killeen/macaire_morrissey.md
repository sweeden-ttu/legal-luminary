# Executive Summary

Morrissey 'Mac' Macaire is running as a write-in candidate for an at-large seat on the Killeen City Council in the May 2026 election. Macaire is a former officer with the Killeen Police Department. His candidacy is accompanied by a notable controversy detailed in a Killeen Daily Herald report, which states he was previously dismissed from the police force in 2016 for impeding a sexual assault investigation into another officer, though he was later reinstated. The provided information did not contain details on his campaign priorities or any endorsements.
