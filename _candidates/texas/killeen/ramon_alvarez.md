# Candidate Profile Summary

Ramon Gabriel Alvarez is an At-Large Councilmember for the City of Killeen, Texas, whose term is set to expire in 2026. In March 2026, he was selected by the City Council to serve as Mayor Pro Tem, acting as mayor in the mayor's absence. Despite his incumbency, Alvarez is not running for reelection in the May 2, 2026 general election. His political platform has focused on reducing 'crime and grime,' promoting downtown revitalization, fostering economic development, and improving overall city services and quality of life, with a particular emphasis on public safety and addressing blight in Killeen's urban core. He was mentioned in a local news report regarding unevidenced social media allegations from another candidate, though he did not comment on the matter.

# Personal History And Community Ties

Ramon Alvarez has deep roots in Killeen, having grown up in the downtown area of the city. This lifelong connection to the community has significantly shaped his professional and political endeavors. His identity as a lifelong Texan is also reflected in his career as a licensed Texas Realtor. His decision to co-found Nolan Creek Builders was influenced by his local upbringing, and his political priorities often center on the revitalization and improvement of Downtown Killeen, the neighborhood where he was raised.
