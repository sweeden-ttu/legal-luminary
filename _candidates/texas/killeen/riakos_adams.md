  Riakos L. Adams is a retired United States Army Major, disabled veteran, and public servant who is currently running for the Mayor of Killeen, Texas. After a 22-year military career, Adams became deeply involved in local civic leadership. His political career in Killeen includes appointments to the City Council in 2022 and 2023, and an election as an At-Large council member in 2024. He is currently running for Mayor in the upcoming election.
