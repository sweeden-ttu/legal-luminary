# Candidate Profile Summary

Riakos L. Adams is a retired United States Army Major, disabled veteran, and public servant who is currently the Mayor of Killeen, Texas. After a 22-year military career, Adams became deeply involved in local civic leadership. His political career in Killeen includes appointments to the City Council in 2022 and 2023, an election as an At-Large council member in 2024, and his selection as Mayor Pro Tem in 2025. In February 2026, he was sworn in as Mayor following the resignation of his predecessor and is currently running for the office in an upcoming election.

# Biographical Background

Riakos L. Adams is a lifelong public servant with a distinguished military and civic background. He enlisted in the U.S. Army in 1994 and was later commissioned as an officer in 2000 through the Howard University ROTC program. He retired in January 2017 with the rank of Major after over 22 years of service, earning awards such as the Bronze Star Medal and the Military Outstanding Volunteer Service Medal. Adams holds a Bachelor's degree in Political Science from the University of the District of Columbia, a Master's in Business Management from Webster University, and has completed doctoral coursework in Human Services. His commitment to service continued after his military retirement through extensive community involvement. He has served as President of Killeen Volunteers, Inc., Executive Director of the Central Texas Youth Volunteer Corps, and held positions on the Bell County Child Welfare Board and Killeen ISD advisory councils. He is a Life Member of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity and a member of numerous veteran service organizations. His political service includes representing Killeen on the Killeen–Temple Metropolitan Planning Organization and holding leadership roles in the Texas Association of Black City Council Members and the National League of Cities.

# Public Safety Stance

Riakos Adams's platform on public safety centers on three main pillars: ensuring first responders are fully resourced to handle their duties effectively, strengthening prevention efforts and partnerships with mental health services to address root causes of crime, and improving communication and visibility of law enforcement to build and maintain community trust and confidence.

# Infrastructure And Growth Plans

Riakos Adams's approach to managing Killeen's growth, which he terms 'Smart Growth,' involves a focus on infrastructure and resilience. His plans include fixing roads and improving traffic flow to ease congestion. He also prioritizes long-term planning for water and utility needs to support a growing population. A key component of his strategy is ensuring that new development pays its fair share for the infrastructure it requires. Furthermore, he plans to proactively address drainage issues and residential flooding, and emphasizes the importance of involving residents earlier in the development and planning processes.

# Neighborhood And Quality Of Life Initiatives

To strengthen Killeen's neighborhoods and improve residents' quality of life, Riakos Adams plans to focus on several key areas. He advocates for responsive city services that promptly address citizen concerns. His platform includes fair and consistent code enforcement to maintain neighborhood standards. He also aims to ensure clean public spaces and create parks that residents will actively use. A central part of his strategy is to foster neighborhood-level leadership and create more opportunities for resident engagement in local governance.

# Economic Development Strategy

Riakos Adams's vision for Killeen's economy is one that is diverse, inclusive, and beneficial for all residents. His strategy includes robust support for small businesses, which he sees as the backbone of the local economy. He also emphasizes workforce development to equip residents with the skills needed for good jobs. To attract new employers, he plans to invest in necessary infrastructure. A critical element of his economic plan is close coordination with Fort Hood to leverage its economic impact and to provide comprehensive support for military families living in Killeen.

# Youth Empowerment Focus

Riakos Adams identifies youth development and empowerment as a core city priority. His platform calls for a coordinated city-wide strategy focused on young people. This strategy includes initiatives designed to build leadership skills, expand pathways into the workforce, strengthen mentorship programs connecting youth with experienced community members, and create meaningful opportunities for civic engagement to prepare them for future leadership roles.
