# Executive Summary

David Bass is a candidate for the Killeen City Council, District 3, in the special election scheduled for May 2, 2026. A retired U.S. Army veteran and a Killeen resident for over 30 years, Bass is running on a platform focused on several key areas. His priorities include bringing well-paying jobs to the city and continuing the momentum of downtown revitalization. He advocates for strong support for emergency services and veterans. Regarding urban growth, such as the 1,300-acre 'Donut Hole' development, Bass emphasizes a need for compromise and insists that infrastructure must be in place before large-scale projects proceed. He also calls for robust public engagement through district-level meetings for major initiatives like the city hall project. Beyond his council candidacy, Bass is a prominent advocate for medical cannabis, having founded Texas Veterans for Medical Marijuana and worked with state legislators on Texas’s Compassionate Use Program.

# Stance On Development And Infrastructure

David Bass's position on development in Killeen is centered on compromise and ensuring infrastructure is adequate to support new growth. Regarding contentious issues like the 1,300-acre "Donut Hole," he states that "compromise is the key." He expresses significant concern about "building a gigantic housing addition... but the infrastructure is not there to support it." As a concrete example, he points to access concerns around the new Chaparral High School, highlighting the existing two-lane road, the need for sidewalks, and improved traffic controls as infrastructure that must be addressed before large-scale development proceeds.

# Advocacy For Medical Marijuana

David Bass is a significant proponent of medical marijuana, having founded the organization 'Texas Veterans for Medical Marijuana'. He has actively worked with state legislators, such as Rep. Brad Buckley and Sen. Pete Flores, on the Texas Compassionate Use Program, which was established in 2015. Bass, a retired U.S. Army veteran, holds a legal medical cannabis prescription and uses his platform to discourage illicit purchases, highlighting the availability of legal alternatives. His personal journey on this issue is notable; he has transitioned from an Army officer who once disciplined soldiers for marijuana use to a vocal champion for its medical benefits, a shift documented in local news features.

# Views On Citizen Engagement

David Bass advocates for robust citizen engagement, particularly for large-scale municipal projects. He believes city officials should proactively seek public input to avoid past failures. Citing the example of the failed city hall bond project, Bass proposes a strategy where officials "go to each district and we have meetings with the citizens… and we make sure that we hear input from everybody on these big projects." This approach emphasizes a district-by-district method to ensure all voices across the city are heard before major decisions are made.
