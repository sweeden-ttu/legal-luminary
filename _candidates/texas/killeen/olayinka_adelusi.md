# Candidate Overview

Olayinka (Adelusi) Adelusi is a candidate for an at-large seat on the Killeen City Council in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. His campaign platform emphasizes several key areas: ensuring public safety, welfare, and prosperity; improving community infrastructure such as parks, schools, and streets; and fostering economic development. A major priority for his campaign is to refocus the city's partnership with the Greater Killeen Chamber of Commerce to stimulate local economic growth and enhance residents' quality of life. He also aims to leverage underutilized resources to address homelessness in the city. As of this review, no public endorsements from credible sources have been identified for his campaign.

# Campaign Platform And Priorities

Adelusi Olayinka's campaign for Killeen City Council is built on several key pillars. A central theme is enhancing the overall well-being and prosperity of the city's residents. His platform emphasizes public safety, which he refers to as "security," along with the general welfare and prosperity of the community. He plans to achieve this by improving fundamental city infrastructure, including parks, schools, streets, and neighborhoods, with the goal of creating a Killeen where every resident can thrive. Economic development is another major priority, with a specific strategy to refocus the city's partnership with the Greater Killeen Chamber of Commerce. This collaboration aims to not only stimulate the local economy but also directly improve the quality of life for residents. Additionally, Olayinka has highlighted his intention to address homelessness by leveraging currently underutilized resources within the city.
