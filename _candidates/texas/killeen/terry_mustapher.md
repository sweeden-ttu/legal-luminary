# Candidate Profile Summary

Terry Mustapher is a candidate for the Killeen City Council District 3 seat in a special election scheduled for May 2, 2026. He is one of three candidates seeking to fill the vacancy. A resident of the Killeen area for approximately 10 years, Mustapher has a background in the nonprofit sector, specifically with an organization providing peer support to veterans. His recent political activity includes an unsuccessful run for Bell County Democratic Party chair in March 2026. While his platform for the party chair position emphasized themes of fairness and integrity, specific policy priorities for his City Council campaign are not detailed in the available sources. No controversies or endorsements related to his current campaign have been reported.

# Election Information

## Office Sought

Killeen City Councilmember

## District

3

## Election Date

May 2, 2026

## Election Type

Special Election

## Reason For Election

To fill the vacant seat of former councilwoman Nina Cobb after she unexpectedly resigned.


# Candidate Background

## Residency Details

## Professional Experience

Based on his LinkedIn profile as reported by the Killeen Daily Herald, Terry Mustapher has worked at 'Bring Everyone Into the Zone.' The nature of this work is tied to the organization's nonprofit services.

## Nonprofit Work

Terry Mustapher's nonprofit involvement is with 'Bring Everyone Into the Zone,' an organization that facilitates a number of services, including providing peer support for veterans.
