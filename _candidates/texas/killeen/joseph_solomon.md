# Candidate Profile Summary

Joseph Solomon is a candidate for Mayor of Killeen, Texas, in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. A military veteran with over 35 years of community advocacy, Solomon has served as the Killeen City Councilmember for District 2 and is the long-time Executive Director of the nonprofit Refuge Corporation. His campaign platform focuses on enhancing public safety, supporting first responders, expanding programs for youth and seniors, promoting transparency in government, maintaining a balanced budget, and fostering economic growth. His candidacy has been marked by a controversy where he resigned from his council seat on January 30, 2026, to run for mayor but chose to "hold over" in the position, a move criticized as a violation of the Killeen City Charter but which Solomon defends as compliant with state law.

# Biographical Background

Joseph Solomon is a military veteran with an extensive background in community service spanning over 35 years. Since 1997, he has served as the Executive Director of the Refuge Corporation, a nonprofit organization that is a partner agency of the United Way of Central Texas and the Combined Federal Campaign. Through his leadership at the Refuge Corporation, Solomon has spearheaded numerous initiatives aimed at addressing community needs, including homelessness, women's and men's shelters, substance abuse and addiction recovery, mobile food pantry distributions, and various outreach programs supporting schools and students. He has a particular focus on combating food insecurity and the existence of food deserts within the community, fostering partnerships with the Central Texas Food Bank and Feeding America. In addition to his nonprofit work, Solomon has a history of public service. He currently serves as the Killeen City Councilmember for District 2 and is a board member for the Hill Country Transit District (The HOP). He previously served as an elected council member in Copperas Cove and has also been a campus pastor and elder at the Christian House of Prayer.

# Controversies

## Issue

Resignation and "Hold Over" on City Council

## Description

A controversy arose in early 2026 when Joseph Solomon resigned from his District 2 city council seat on January 30, 2026, to run for mayor, which created a vacancy as of February 7. However, Solomon chose to “hold over” and remain in his council position. According to a Killeen Daily Herald report, this action violates the Killeen City Charter but is compliant with Texas state law. Mayor Pro Tem Riakos Adams, who is also a mayoral candidate, made the same decision. Both actions were criticized by former councilman Jose Segarra. Solomon defended his choice not to step down, arguing that it is permissible under state law.

# Council Seat Resignation

Joseph Solomon resigned from his District 2 City Council seat on January 30, 2026, in order to run for Mayor of Killeen. According to city documents, this created a vacancy as of February 7, 2026. Following his resignation, the Killeen City Council was expected to vote on calling a special election to fill the seat. However, Solomon chose to “hold over” in his position, a move that the Killeen Daily Herald reported as being in violation of the Killeen City Charter but in compliance with state law. Solomon defended his decision, arguing it was permitted under state law. Mayor Pro Tem Riakos Adams, also a mayoral candidate, made a similar decision, and both were criticized by former councilman Jose Segarra.
