profile_summary: |
  Nicholas Guajardo, a 37-year-old U.S. Army veteran and human resources professional, is a candidate for one of the three at-large seats on the Killeen City Council in the May 2, 2026, general election. With an MBA from Texas A&M University–Central Texas and over 11 years of residency in Killeen, Guajardo is running a campaign heavily focused on economic development. His key priorities include attracting new industries and jobs, leveraging modern tools like AI and SEO to market the city to site selectors, reducing crime through local employment, and expanding opportunities in blue-collar and trade fields. He also aims to strengthen public safety and improve Killeen's overall image and community pride. This is his first documented run for public office.
