# Executive Summary

Nicholas Guajardo, a 37-year-old U.S. Army veteran and human resources professional, is a candidate for one of the three at-large seats on the Killeen City Council in the May 2, 2026, general election. With an MBA from Texas A&M University–Central Texas and over 11 years of residency in Killeen, Guajardo is running a campaign heavily focused on economic development. His key priorities include attracting new industries and jobs, leveraging modern tools like AI and SEO to market the city to site selectors, reducing crime through local employment, and expanding opportunities in blue-collar and trade fields. He also aims to strengthen public safety and improve Killeen's overall image and community pride. This is his first documented run for public office.

# Campaign Priorities

Nicholas Guajardo's campaign for a Killeen City Council at-large seat is heavily centered on economic and community development, with a primary focus on job creation. His platform's core tenets include attracting new industries and high-quality jobs to Killeen, retaining local talent to prevent skilled workers from leaving the city, and ensuring that local spending benefits the community. Guajardo also advocates for expanding opportunities in blue-collar and trade professions. Public safety is another key pillar of his campaign, which he links directly to economic opportunity. His campaign tagline, "Focused on jobs, safety, and a stronger future for Killeen," encapsulates his main priorities.

# Economic Development Strategy

Nicholas Guajardo proposes a modern, technology-driven strategy for Killeen's economic growth. A central component of his plan is to leverage modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) approaches and Search Engine Optimization (SEO) marketing techniques to proactively attract businesses and site selectors. His goal is to improve how Killeen is presented to outside investors and companies looking to relocate or expand. By enhancing the city's digital footprint and marketability, he aims to attract new industries and create a more robust job market. This strategy reflects his background in human resources and his MBA, aiming to apply contemporary business development tactics to municipal economic policy.

# Stance On Community And Crime

Nicholas Guajardo's perspective on community improvement and crime reduction is rooted in his focus on economic opportunity. He believes that providing stable, local employment is a key factor in reducing crime rates. By creating a healthier job market and more opportunities for residents, he argues that the city can address some of the root causes of criminal activity. In addition to this economic approach, Guajardo calls for residents to have more civic pride and works towards improving the city's overall image. He sees a stronger sense of community and a positive city identity as integral to fostering a safer and more prosperous Killeen.
