# Executive Summary

Chet Southworth is a civil attorney and the lead attorney at Southworth Legal, P.C., running for an At-large seat on the Killeen City Council in the nonpartisan general election on May 2, 2026. His professional practice areas include family law, wills, and mediation. According to his filing documents reported by local news, he has resided in Killeen for over eight years. While his candidacy and professional background are clearly established, there is limited publicly available information regarding his specific campaign platform, priorities, or any official endorsements. His campaign finance reports are on file with the City of Killeen, but detailed policy stances have not been published on major nonpartisan election information sites like Ballotpedia as of the current date.
