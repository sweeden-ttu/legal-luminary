  Jose Segarra, a former mayor of Killeen and long-time real estate broker, is a nonpartisan candidate for Mayor of Killeen in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. He is running against two other candidates, Riakos Adams and Joseph Solomon. Segarra's campaign is centered on his prior experience, arguing that he can be more effective in a second term without a 'learning curve.' His key priorities include strengthening trust and transparency in local government, attracting economic investment and opportunities by leveraging his existing relationships, and achieving measurable progress in public safety, infrastructure, and smart growth. He positions himself as a business owner with expertise in real estate and a deep understanding of the city's needs.
