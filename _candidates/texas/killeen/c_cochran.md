# Candidate Summary

Camron Cochran, who identifies as Dr. Camron Cochran, is a candidate for the Killeen City Council, District 3, in the special election scheduled for May 2, 2026. His campaign materials emphasize his community service and volunteer work with organizations such as the USO, The Mission Continues, and Habitat for Humanity. However, a detailed platform with specific policy positions was not accessible in the reviewed sources. Cochran is a locally known figure who has run for office multiple times and has been involved in several public controversies. These include a 2024 trespass citation related to a dispute with a downtown business, being removed from city council meetings on at least two occasions, and a heated exchange at a 2024 council meeting where he reportedly made disparaging remarks to a disabled Army veteran. More recently, in April 2026, he was involved in a social media controversy involving accusations of using fake profiles. While he has filed the necessary ballot application, treasurer appointment, and a campaign finance report for the period ending March 31, 2026, no official endorsements were identified in the provided materials.

# Key Controversies

Camron Cochran has been involved in several public controversies. In April 2026, he publicly accused at-large Councilmember Ramon Alvarez of using fake social media names to harass people, an accusation that news reports note has also been leveled against Cochran himself. He is also known for frequent and public disputes with other council members and downtown business owners. In 2024, he was criticized for remarks made to a disabled Army veteran during a city council meeting, where he reportedly called the Army and Navy "cowards." Additionally, he received a trespass citation in May 2024 following a dispute involving alleged harassment at a downtown business called "The Mint." Reports also indicate he has been removed from Killeen City Council meetings on at least two occasions.
