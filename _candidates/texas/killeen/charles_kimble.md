# Executive Summary

Charles “Chuck” Kimble, the former Killeen Police Chief with over three decades of public service, is the sole and unopposed candidate for the Killeen City Council District 2 seat in a special election scheduled for May 2, 2026. Having filed on February 18, 2026, to fill the vacant seat, Kimble faces no challengers. Consequently, the city has indicated the election for District 2 is canceled, making him the presumptive council member. His platform focuses on leveraging his experience to enhance public safety, improve city services and infrastructure, and ensure responsible planning for Killeen's growth.

# Professional Background

Charles 'Chuck' Kimble has a career spanning 32 years in public service, culminating in his role as the Police Chief for the City of Killeen. He announced his retirement from the Killeen Police Department on December 6, 2022, with his last day being January 27, 2023. During his tenure as chief, he was responsible for several key initiatives, including the creation of a Homeless Outreach Team and contributing to the concept of a downtown revitalization-focused unit. After his retirement from the KPD, Kimble continued to serve in public safety as an interim police chief in Jarrell. He also worked as a city consultant and in the private sector with Homevets Realty. In 2026, he filed to run for the vacant District 2 City Council seat in Killeen, a position he is poised to take unopposed.

# Campaign Priorities

Charles Kimble's campaign for the Killeen City Council District 2 seat is centered on providing 'steady, experienced leadership' that prioritizes public safety, families, and quality of life in every decision. For District 2 specifically, he advocates for focused attention on the everyday issues that directly impact residents' lives, including neighborhood safety, responsive city services, and robust code enforcement. He also emphasizes the need for infrastructure development that keeps pace with the city's growth. On a city-wide level, Kimble's platform includes strengthening partnerships related to public safety, enhancing communication and transparency from the city government, and pursuing smart, responsible planning for the future. His background as the former Killeen Police Chief informs his approach, where he previously initiated programs like the Homeless Outreach Team and a unit focused on downtown revitalization.

# Community Engagement Plan

Charles Kimble plans to maintain communication and accountability with the residents of District 2 through a strategy of sustained engagement. A key part of his platform is to improve overall communication and transparency between the city council and the public.
