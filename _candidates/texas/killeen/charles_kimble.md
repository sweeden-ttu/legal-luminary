  Charles “Chuck” Kimble, the former Killeen Police Chief with over three decades of public service, is the sole and unopposed candidate for the Killeen City Council District 2 seat in a special election scheduled for May 2, 2026. Having filed on February 18, 2026, to fill the vacant seat, Kimble faces no challengers. Consequently, the city has indicated the election for District 2 is canceled, making him the presumptive council member. His platform focuses on leveraging his experience to enhance public safety, improve city services and infrastructure, and ensure responsible planning for Killeen's growth.
