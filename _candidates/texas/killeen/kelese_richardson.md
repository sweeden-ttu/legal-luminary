# Executive Summary

Kelese Richardson is a confirmed nonpartisan candidate for one of three At-large seats on the Killeen City Council in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. A resident of the Killeen area for approximately 13 years, she works in customer service. Her candidacy is officially on the ballot, as confirmed by her filed application with the City of Killeen. Richardson has secured an endorsement from the Killeen Professional Firefighters Association. As of April 2026, detailed information regarding her specific campaign platform and policy priorities is not widely available in the public domain, with no official campaign website located in the provided sources.
