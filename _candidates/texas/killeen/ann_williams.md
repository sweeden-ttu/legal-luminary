profile_summary: |
  Dr. Beverly Ann Williams is a candidate for the Killeen City Council At-Large seat in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. A U.S. Army veteran and a resident of Killeen for approximately 25 to 30 years, Dr. Williams has a significant background in mental health and faith-based counseling. Her career includes serving as a chaplain and counselor at Gatesville prison, working with at-risk youth, and advocating for sexual assault victims with Families in Crisis. She has also served on the board of the International Institute of Faith-Based Counseling, hosted the local television show “The Let’s Get Real Show” for over a decade, and founded her own counseling center. Her campaign is fundamentally centered on community engagement and ensuring that every resident of Killeen has a 'voice' in determining the city's future.
