# Executive Summary

Dominic Quoc Vo, a 43-year-old local business owner, is a candidate for one of three Killeen City Council At-large seats in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. An immigrant who arrived in the U.S. in 1995, Vo has been a Killeen resident since 2012 and owns several local businesses. His campaign platform focuses on three main areas: fiscal responsibility, proactive infrastructure management, and public safety. Key proposals include advocating for state reimbursement to cover a $13 million budget deficit caused by disabled veteran tax exemptions, implementing a 'Line-Item First' budget approach, and shifting from reactive to scheduled infrastructure repairs, particularly for water systems and key road expansions. He has officially filed for a place on the ballot and is actively campaigning, with no notable controversies reported in local media.

## Background Summary

Dominic Quoc Vo immigrated to the United States in April 1995 at the age of 13. His family came to the U.S. through the Humanitarian Operation program. This was after his father had spent seven and a half years in a re-education camp in Vietnam. After arriving, he grew up in Iowa and has been a resident of Killeen, Texas since 2012.

## Education

Vo graduated from Morningside University in 2004 with a Bachelor of Science degree in International Business and Marketing.

## Professional Career

He is a small business owner in the Killeen area, where he operates multiple enterprises, including an insurance agency, a real estate brokerage, a nail salon, and a coworking space.

## Family And Community

Dominic Vo is married to his wife, Hannah, and they have two children, a son named Oliver and a daughter named Olivia. He is an active member of the St. Paul Chong Hasang Catholic Church located in the neighboring city of Harker Heights.

# Campaign Platform Overview

Dominic Vo's campaign for Killeen City Council At-Large is built on several core themes, including fiscal responsibility, infrastructure reliability, and robust public safety. As a small business owner, he emphasizes supporting local entrepreneurs and attracting investment to ensure Killeen is prosperous. His platform advocates for a 'servant mind' approach to leadership, aiming to make the city a better place to grow and raise families. Key priorities highlighted in his messaging include implementing a transparent, 'Line-Item First' budget, proactively addressing the city's aging infrastructure, and ensuring public safety departments are well-funded and competitive. He also stresses the need for improved transparency from the city council, particularly concerning spending and utility rate justifications.

# Policy On Fiscal Responsibility

Dominic Vo's platform places a strong emphasis on fiscal responsibility, centered on a 'Line-Item First' approach to the 2026–2027 city budget to ensure transparent and justified spending. A major component of his fiscal policy is addressing the approximately $13 million annual budget shortfall caused by the state's 100% Disabled Veteran tax exemption. Vo proposes to work directly with state legislators to demand that Texas fully reimburses the city of Killeen for this revenue loss, rather than placing the burden on other local taxpayers. He also advocates for greater transparency in city finances, specifically citing the need for the council to be clearer with residents about rising water and sewer bills. His financial priorities also include allocating necessary funding for public safety initiatives.

# Policy On Infrastructure

Dominic Vo's infrastructure policy focuses on shifting Killeen from reactive repairs to proactive, scheduled maintenance. He specifically calls for a long-term replacement plan for aging water lines, with an initial focus on North and Central Killeen, to improve water reliability. For transportation, he advocates for smart traffic solutions, including fast-tracking the completion of the Bunny Trail and Zephyr Road expansions. Furthermore, Vo pledges to hold the city accountable for the $128 million Water & Wastewater Master Plan, ensuring it remains on schedule and within budget. He also believes that any new development must be matched by the city's infrastructure capacity to support it, preventing strain on existing systems.

# Policy On Economic Development

As a small business owner himself, operating an insurance agency, real estate brokerage, nail salon, and coworking space, Dominic Vo's economic development strategy is centered on supporting local entrepreneurs and businesses. He lists 'supporting local businesses' as one of his top priorities. His approach involves creating a practical, business-friendly environment and reducing barriers to investment. The underlying goal of his economic policy is to ensure that new and existing businesses can create jobs and strengthen Killeen's local economy, contributing to the city's overall prosperity.

# Policy On Public Safety

Dominic Vo's public safety platform includes specific funding and personnel retention strategies. He pledges to prioritize funding for Killeen's Real-Time Crime Center to enhance law enforcement capabilities. Additionally, he is committed to ensuring competitive pay for the city's police and fire department personnel. The goal of this policy is to retain talented and experienced public safety officers and firefighters, thereby maintaining a high level of safety and emergency response for the community.

# Policy On Homelessness

Dominic Vo proposes a budget-conscious and practical approach to addressing homelessness in Killeen, emphasizing job stability as a primary solution. Rather than focusing on constructing new shelters, he suggests a more creative and potentially cost-effective solution: utilizing the city's many existing empty buildings. He believes these vacant properties could be repurposed to provide shelter and services. This 'jobs-first' and asset-reuse strategy reflects his desire to find practical solutions that are fiscally responsible for the city.

# Views On Government Transparency

Dominic Vo is a strong advocate for increased transparency from the Killeen City Council. He believes the city government needs to improve its communication with residents, particularly concerning financial matters that directly affect them. He has specifically called out the need for the city to 'be more transparent' about the reasons behind rising water and sewer bills, arguing that citizens deserve clear explanations for such cost increases. This stance is part of his broader platform of ensuring accountability and open governance in city projects and budgeting.
