# Executive Summary

Myrna Malic-Banzon is a confirmed candidate running for one of three At-large seats on the Killeen City Council in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. Her candidacy is officially registered, with an application filed on February 13, 2026, and a Candidate/Officeholder Campaign Finance Report (Form COH) submitted on April 2, 2026. Originally from the Philippines, Malic-Banzon settled in Killeen, Texas, through a military connection. As of April 2026, she has not completed Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection survey, and her campaign's social media focuses on general community engagement rather than specific policy planks. No formal endorsements or public controversies have been identified. She is one of several candidates in a crowded nonpartisan field for the available seats.

# Biographical Background

Myrna Malic-Banzon's personal history traces back to the Philippines, where she is described as having grown up in a 'privileged lifestyle.' Her journey to Central Texas was facilitated by a military connection, which led her to settle in Killeen, where she currently resides. This background, bridging her life in the Philippines with her present in Killeen, is a notable aspect of her personal story. For campaign-related inquiries, her official contact email is listed as myrna4killeen@gmail.com.
