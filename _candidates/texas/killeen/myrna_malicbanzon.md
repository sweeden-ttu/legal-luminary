As of April 2026, she has not completed Ballotpedia's Candidate Connection survey, and her campaign's social media focuses on general community engagement rather than specific policy planks. No formal endorsements or public controversies have been identified. She is one of several candidates in a crowded nonpartisan field for the available seats.
