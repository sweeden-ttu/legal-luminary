# Candidate Profile Summary

Sue “Neesy” Croft is a candidate for one of three at-large seats on the Killeen City Council in the May 2, 2026 election. A business owner and resident of Killeen for over three decades, Croft is running in a crowded field of 15 candidates. Her campaign platform centers on improving the "quality of life" for residents, with a specific focus on development oversight, road maintenance, and enhancing communication between the city and its citizens. She has positioned herself as a “watchdog” who will ensure residents are informed about new developments in their neighborhoods.

# Campaign Platform And Priorities

Sue “Neesy” Croft, a business owner and resident of Killeen for over three decades, has centered her campaign for a City Council at-large seat on several key priorities. # Stance On Development

Sue Croft has made development a central issue of her campaign, positioning herself as a 'watchdog' for the residents of Killeen. Her stance is heavily influenced by her experience as a resident of the 'Donut Hole,' a 1,300-acre area in south Killeen where residents have frequently clashed with the city and developers over zoning issues. She pledges to bring transparency to the development process, promising to ensure that residents understand what is being built in their neighborhoods. This suggests a pro-resident, oversight-focused approach to new development and zoning changes within the city.
