profile_summary: |
  Delsina West is a candidate running for the Killeen City Council At-large in the general election scheduled for May 2, 2026. Professionally, she is a certified Wellness Practitioner, Personal Trainer, and Nutrition Coach based in Killeen, Texas, where she operates her business, DWest Coaching. Her background includes a Master Coach certification from Precision Nutrition (L2), a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, and a Certified Wellness Practitioner (CWP) designation. She is affiliated with the National Wellness Institute and PMI and is proficient in both Spanish and English. According to her campaign materials, her vision for Killeen is centered on 'Community Connection, Transparent Governance, and Responsible Growth.' Her key priorities include Health & Wellness, Economic Development, Infrastructure, and Community Engagement.
