profile_summary: |
  Judge Paul LePak currently serves as the Judge of the 264th Judicial District Court in Bell County, a position he has held since his appointment by Governor Greg Abbott in 2018. In the 2026 election cycle, he is seeking the bench of the 169th District Court.

  ### Academic Background
  Judge LePak earned his Juris Doctor from the **Villanova University School of Law** in 1990. He completed his undergraduate studies at **Marquette University**, where he received a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science in 1987.

  ### Professional History
  With over 30 years of legal experience, Judge LePak spent nearly three decades as a distinguished litigator in private practice before joining the judiciary. His practice specialized in complex civil litigation, family law, and criminal defense. He is a Fellow of the Texas Bar Foundation and has held leadership roles as the president and treasurer of the Bell County Criminal Defense Lawyers Association.

  Known for his disciplined approach to the law, Judge LePak has been entrusted by the Supreme Court of Texas with special judicial assignments, reflecting his reputation for impartiality and legal expertise.
