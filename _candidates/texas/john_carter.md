# John R. Carter#

## Office
U.S. House of Representatives, Texas District 31 (formerly District 11 prior to 2023 redistricting)

## Biography
John R. Carter is the U.S. Representative for Texas's 31st congressional district, serving since 2023 (previously District 11 from 2003-2023). He is a member of the Republican Party.
