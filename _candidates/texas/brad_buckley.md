# Brad Buckley#

## Office
Texas House of Representatives, District 54

## Biography
Brad Buckley is a Republican member of the Texas House of Representatives representing District 54, which includes Bell County and part of Lampasas County. He was first elected in 2018 and is currently serving his fourth term (89th Legislature). He serves as Chairman of the House Public Education committee and the House Select Committee on Educational Opportunity and Enrichment. He is a veterinarian who opened his practice in Killeen in 1993. He served on the Killeen ISD school board and is a former Chair of the Greater Killeen Chamber of Commerce. He and his wife Susan live in Salado and have three children: Emily, Erin, and Bo.
