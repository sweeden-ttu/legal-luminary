Democratic U.S. Senate candidate James Talarico has demanded that his opponent, Republican Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton, release communications and files regarding a plea deal offered to a Waco man. The individual was indicted for child sexual abuse, and Talarico is seeking transparency on how the Attorney General's office handled the agreement.
