CBS News Texas reports that Gov. Greg Abbott, seeking a fourth term, urged party unity at the Texas GOP convention in Houston and criticized the top three Democrats on the statewide ballot. He pledged to eliminate school property taxes for most homeowners, require data centers to generate their own power and water, and ban Sharia law, and also mocked Democratic U.S. Senate nominee James Talarico.
