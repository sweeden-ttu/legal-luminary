FOX 7 Austin reports that Brian Keith Fikac, 53, of Holland, was charged with aggravated assault against a public servant, a first-degree felony, after a shooting involving Bell County deputies on Friday, June 19, 2026. Deputies had responded to a 911 domestic-disturbance call at a home in the 18000 block of FM 2268 east of Holland and fired after Fikac displayed what they believed was a firearm; a justice of the peace set his bond at $500,000.
