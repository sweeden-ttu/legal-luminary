## Statewide Runoff Highlights (May 26, 2026)

Texas voters returned to the polls on Tuesday for the primary runoff elections, resolving several high-stakes contests and setting the stage for a competitive general election in November.

### U.S. Senate (Republican)
In the most closely watched race of the night, Attorney General **Ken Paxton** defeated incumbent Senator **John Cornyn** for the Republican nomination.

*   **Ken Paxton:** ~63.8%
*   **John Cornyn:** ~36.2%

Paxton will face Democrat **James Talarico** in the November general election. This race is expected to be one of the most expensive and closely watched Senate contests in the nation.

### Attorney General (Republican)
With Ken Paxton vacating the Attorney General's office to run for Senate, the Republican nomination was won by **Mayes Middleton**.

*   **Mayes Middleton:** 55.2%
*   **Chip Roy:** 44.8%

Middleton will face Democrat **Nathan Johnson** in the general election.

### Lieutenant Governor (Democratic)
**Vikki Goodwin** emerged victorious in the Democratic runoff for Lieutenant Governor.

*   **Vikki Goodwin:** 67.8%
*   **Marcos Velez:** 32.2%

Goodwin will challenge incumbent Republican **Dan Patrick** this November.

### Railroad Commissioner (Republican)
In an upset, **Bo French** defeated incumbent **Jim Wright**.

*   **Bo French:** 50.6%
*   **Jim Wright:** 49.4%

## Judicial Runoffs and Other Notable Races
The runoffs also settled several judicial nominations and U.S. House races:

*   **Bell County Justice of the Peace, Pct 4 Pl 2 (Democratic):** **Jessica A. Gonzalez** defeated Latasha Carroway Quarles to advance to the general election against Republican Beatrice Cox.
*   **U.S. House District 18 (Democratic):** **Christian Menefee** defeated incumbent Al Green.
*   **U.S. House District 33 (Democratic):** **Colin Allred** defeated Julie Johnson.

## November General Election
With the primary process now complete, candidates will pivot to the general election on **November 3, 2026**. Texas voters will decide on a full slate of statewide offices, judicial seats, and legislative positions.
