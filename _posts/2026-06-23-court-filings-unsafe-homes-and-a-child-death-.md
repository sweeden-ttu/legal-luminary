Reports concerning the state of child welfare systems indicate ongoing scrutiny regarding residential safety standards. The discussion centers on whether systemic improvements have adequately addressed critical risks faced by children in care.

Recent reviews note that efforts within the foster care system have focused on improving caseworker training and managing caseload sizes. These measures represent attempts to enhance professional support for those involved in child protection services.

Despite these reported advancements, unsafe living conditions for children remain a persistent concern across the state. Furthermore, reports continue to highlight instances of child death, suggesting that systemic challenges persist despite recent reforms.
