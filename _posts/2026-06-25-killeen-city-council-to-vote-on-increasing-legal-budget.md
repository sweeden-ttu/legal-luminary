The Killeen City Council is set to meet to consider approving additional funds to defend itself in an ongoing lawsuit with Bell County over a voter-approved marijuana decriminalization measure. To fight the lawsuit, the Killeen City Council originally hired a legal team with a budget not to exceed $100,000. However, according to the meeting agenda, more funds are now needed for the city to continue its defense in the case.
