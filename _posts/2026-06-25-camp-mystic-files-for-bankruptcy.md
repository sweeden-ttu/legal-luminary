Camp Mystic has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy relief according to recent court records. The case was filed in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court, approximately a year following a tragic flooding incident. This legal maneuver halts pending civil litigation and provides the camp with time to restructure its finances amid ongoing scrutiny.
