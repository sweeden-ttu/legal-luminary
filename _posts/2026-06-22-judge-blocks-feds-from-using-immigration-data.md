A federal judge recently issued a ruling regarding the use of certain databases in voter eligibility checks. The court addressed Texas' utilization of the SAVE database, which flagged several individuals who were actually citizens. Officials argued that these specific data checks infringed upon established privacy rights and violated voting entitlements.
