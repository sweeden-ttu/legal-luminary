The owner of a historic brick manufacturing company located in Bastrop has filed a $29 million lawsuit alleging systemic fraud. Court documents indicate that the business owner claims deceptive practices by partners led to significant financial damages and jeopardized the company's legacy. Legal experts suggest that this complex civil litigation will require an extensive discovery process as both sides prepare their arguments.
