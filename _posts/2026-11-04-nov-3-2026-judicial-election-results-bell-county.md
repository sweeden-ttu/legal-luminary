Democrats won every statewide judicial seat on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot: four on the Texas Supreme Court, three on the Court of Criminal Appeals and three on the Fifteenth Court of Appeals, unseating each Republican incumbent in those races, according to unofficial returns from the [Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html). The Texas Tribune reported that Republicans keep a 5–4 majority on the Supreme Court and that Chief Justice Jimmy Blacklock lost to Third Court of Appeals Justice Maggie Ellis ([The Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/04/texas-supreme-court-criminal-appeals-results-elections-2026/)). Third Court of Appeals Chief Justice Darlene Byrne (D) was re-elected over Cory Liu (R) districtwide ([Texas Secretary of State](https://electionresults.sos.state.tx.us/results.html)).

In Bell County, the Killeen Daily Herald reported that Killeen Mayor Pro Tem Jessica Gonzalez (D) won the race for Justice of the Peace, Precinct 4, Place 2, over Beatrice "Bea" Cox (R), the only contested local judicial race ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/news/local/killeen-mayor-pro-tem-jessica-gonzalez-steamrolls-to-victory-in-bell-county-jp-race/article_a4e03829-e513-4185-a9c0-547fe8c4823e.html)). Every unopposed Bell County judicial candidate was elected, according to the county's unofficial results ([Bell County Elections](https://results.enr.clarityelections.com/TX/Bell/127475/)). The Herald's election-night rundown also reports Democratic wins in both contested county commissioner races and in Texas House District 55 ([Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/centerforpolitics/here-s-a-rundown-of-area-election-results-from-tuesday-night/article_61b4fae6-2d44-4b71-a8c9-cca408c33344.html)).
