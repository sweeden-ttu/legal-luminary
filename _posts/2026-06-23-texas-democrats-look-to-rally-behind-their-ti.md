Texas Democrats are participating in a state convention designed to build momentum for various down-ballot races. The event is intended to generate enthusiasm and support for specific candidates ahead of the general election cycle this November. Prominent national figures, including U.S. Senator Bernie Sanders, are scheduled to address attendees at the gathering.
