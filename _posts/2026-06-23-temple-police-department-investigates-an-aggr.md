06/23/26<br /><br />Temple Police Department investigates an aggravated assault, 1 injured<br /><br /><br />TEMPLE, TX (June 23, 2026) – The Temple Police Department is investigating an aggravated assault that left one individual injured.<br /><br />At around 12:40 a.m., on Tuesday, June 23, officers were dispatched to the 1800 block of Marlandwood Road for reports of a shooting. Upon arrival, officers spoke with the victim, who advised officers that they had heard a loud noise coming from the apartment upstairs. When the victim went to check on the noise, they observed the second-floor apartment door open. The suspect then saw the victim, walked out of the apartment, fired a handgun, and fled on foot.<br /><br />The suspect is described as a black male, approximately 5’ 3” to 5’ 5”, thin build, with short hair. <br /><br />One individual was transported to the hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. No other injuries have been reported at this time. <br /><br />The Temple Police Department Criminal Investigations Division will be on scene investigating. <br /><br />This case is under investigation, and no other information will be released at this time. Anyone with information should contact the Temple Police Department at 254-298-5500 or the Bell County Crime Stoppers at 254-526-8477, where callers can report anonymously. Tips can also be submitted anonymously at bellcountycrimestoppers.com.<br />
