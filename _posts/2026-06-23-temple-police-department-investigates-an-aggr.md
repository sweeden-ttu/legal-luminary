According to a City of Temple release, Temple police were dispatched about 12:40 a.m. on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, to the 1800 block of Marlandwood Road for reports of a shooting. Police say a suspect left a second-floor apartment, fired a handgun and fled on foot; one person was taken to a hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. The case remains under investigation.
