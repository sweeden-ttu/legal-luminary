Rising costs have many Texans facing concerns about affordability. The candidates in the race for governor are pushing different approaches to provide relief. As voters look toward the upcoming election, economic issues remain at the forefront of the political conversation. Some candidates are advocating for broad tax cuts, while others suggest direct relief in the form of $1,500 checks to assist residents struggling with inflation. These proposals highlight the varying strategies candidates are offering to address economic concerns in Texas.
