The Texas Tribune's Alejandra Martinez reports that the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality has proposed rules for reusing treated oilfield "produced water," including spreading it on farmland, and scheduled a public hearing for June 15, 2026. Supporters describe the reuse as a possible response to the state's water shortages, while critics say the proposed rules are too general and do not require testing for contaminants such as radionuclides, PFAS or heavy metals.
