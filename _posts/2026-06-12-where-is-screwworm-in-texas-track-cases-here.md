The New World screwworm represents a significant economic threat to Texas's cattle industry. Officials note that this biological issue carries potential costs amounting to billions of dollars across the state’s agricultural sector.

Statewide monitoring efforts are currently in place to track reported cases of the parasite. These tracking mechanisms allow authorities and ranchers to understand the geographical spread of the infestation.

The continuous reporting system is intended to assist local producers and regulatory bodies in managing outbreaks effectively. This proactive approach helps mitigate potential losses for livestock operations throughout Texas.
