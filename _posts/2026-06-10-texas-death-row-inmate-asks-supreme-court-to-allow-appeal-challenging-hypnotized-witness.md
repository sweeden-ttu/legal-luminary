The Texas Tribune (Ayden Runnels, June 10, 2026) reports that Charles Flores, sentenced to death in 1999 for the murder of Betty Black, has asked the U.S. Supreme Court to let him use Texas's "junk science" law to challenge testimony from a neighbor who was hypnotized by police. Dallas County prosecutors argue he has not shown enough new evidence.
