KBTX's "TDCJ Investigation" section collects the station's reporting on inmate Gonzalo Lopez's 2022 escape from a prison transport near Centerville, which the station says led to five deaths and Lopez's own death. Its May 12, 2023 item marks one year since the escape; earlier items report that Lopez had requested books on survival techniques and weapons before escaping and feature criminal justice expert Mitchel Roth on what went wrong.
