A congressional judiciary committee has requested access to records maintained by a Travis County District Attorney’s office. The demand relates specifically to immigration prosecutions handled within the county. This action suggests an ongoing federal interest in reviewing local enforcement policies and data.

Several members of the Republican party serving on the committee were involved in the request. These officials publicly accused the District Attorney, José Garza, of maintaining certain policies that they believe undermine the rule of law. The focus of the criticism centers on how immigration matters are handled within the county's legal framework.
