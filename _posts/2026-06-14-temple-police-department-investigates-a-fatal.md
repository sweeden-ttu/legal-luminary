According to a City of Temple release, Temple police responded about 7:39 a.m. on Sunday, June 14, 2026, to a single-vehicle crash at W. Adams Avenue and NW HK Dodgen Loop in which the vehicle struck a pole and caught fire. One person was pronounced dead at the scene by Justice of the Peace Rosanne Fisher; police later identified the deceased as 37-year-old Milan Maharjan of Temple. The case remains under investigation.
