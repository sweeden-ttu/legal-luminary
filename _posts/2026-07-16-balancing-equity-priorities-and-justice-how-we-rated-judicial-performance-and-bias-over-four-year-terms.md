## Assessing the Bench: A Four-Year report card for Central Texas Judicial Officers

To ensure transparency and accountability in the Bell County court system, Legal Luminary has conducted a comprehensive assessment of local judicial performance. Over their four-year terms, we evaluated each presiding judge, prosecutor, and attorney to identify potential bias and grade overall performance. This evaluation seeks to empower voters in Killeen, Temple, and Belton ahead of the upcoming election.

### Methodology for Scoring Judicial Performance

Our evaluation process focuses on a rigorous analysis of district court and county court at law cases. We reviewed thousands of case files to measure how each judicial officer balances the strict requirements of Texas law with the pursuit of equity. Key metrics include:

1. **Constitutional Consistency:** Evaluating whether a judge's rulings align with the Texas Penal Code and procedural rules for both felony and misdemeanor cases.
2. **Efficiency and Backlog Management:** Measuring the time from initial indictment to final disposition in each District Court and County Court at Law.
3. **Impartiality and Bias Detection:** Analyzing sentencing disparities and the frequency with which a prosecutor or defense attorney raised objections regarding judicial conduct.

### The Role of Justice of the Peace and Municipal Courts

While high-level felony trials occur in a District Court, the first point of contact for most residents is a Justice of the Peace precinct court or a municipal court in Killeen or Temple. We evaluated these courts on accessibility and procedural fairness. A key focus was whether the presiding judge or candidate demonstrated a commitment to judicial independence rather than corporate balance sheets.

### Looking Ahead to the Election

As Bell County prepares for the upcoming judicial election, this rating system provides a data-driven guide for voters. Understanding how each candidate has administered justice over their term is essential for restoring legitimacy to our local legal system.
