Texas Republicans are actively pursuing both legal avenues and upcoming legislative sessions to close the state's open primary system. Proponents of the change argue that closed primaries will prevent voters from crossing party lines and influencing the outcome of the opposing party's elections. The move comes as part of a broader push to tighten election laws and ensure that only registered party members have a say in selecting their candidates for the general election.
