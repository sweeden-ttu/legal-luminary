The U.S. Supreme Court recently denied an appeal filed by death row inmate Victor Saldaño concerning his intellectual disability claims. Despite the federal ruling, attorneys and experts representing both the state and the accused have reached a consensus on a key procedural matter.

This agreement stipulates that Mr. Saldaño should be permitted to present evidence of his disability before a state criminal appeals court. This means the issue will proceed through the established state legal channels rather than being solely determined at the federal level. The focus remains on ensuring all relevant evidence regarding the inmate's condition is considered by local judicial authorities.
