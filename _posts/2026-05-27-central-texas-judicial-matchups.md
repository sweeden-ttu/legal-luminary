## 2026 Central Texas Judicial Matchups

With the conclusion of the primary runoff elections on May 26, 2026, the field of candidates for judicial offices in Bell County and Killeen is now finalized for the November General Election.

### Justice of the Peace, Precinct 4, Place 2 (Killeen)
This race represents one of the most significant contests in the Killeen area. Following a competitive runoff, the matchups are:

*   **Democrat:** **Jessica A. Gonzalez** (Current Killeen City Councilwoman)
*   **Republican:** **Beatrice "Bea" Cox**

Gonzalez secured the Democratic nomination after defeating Latasha Carroway Quarles in the runoff. Cox advanced unopposed from the Republican primary.

### Bell County Judge (Commissioners Court)
While primarily an administrative role, the County Judge serves a vital judicial function in Bell County.

*   **Republican:** **David Blackburn** (Incumbent)
*   **Democrat:** *None filed*

Judge Blackburn remains the heavy favorite for re-election as no Democratic challenger entered the race.

### Bell County Justice of the Peace, Precinct 2
The Republican nomination for this precinct was decided in the runoff:

*   **Republican:** **Richard Sapp**
*   **Democrat:** *None filed*

Richard Sapp won the Republican runoff against Michael Tice.

## Looking Ahead to November
The General Election will be held on **November 3, 2026**. While several local races are unopposed at the county level, the contest for **Justice of the Peace Precinct 4, Place 2** remains a key focus for Killeen voters.
