**Headline:** Paxton’s office threatens legal action if Big 12 penalizes Texas Tech for playing QB who placed bets

**Byline:** Ayden Runnels, Alex Nguyen

**Date:** 2026-06-11T20:00:28+00:00

**Article Body:**

A Lubbock judge blocked the NCAA from banning quarterback Brendan Sorsby despite an acknowledged gambling addiction. The Texas Tech quarterback, who has a gambling problem, was facing potential penalties from the Big 12 Conference. In response, Texas Tech's athletic director Ken Paxton threatened legal action if the conference penalized the school for Sorsby's involvement.

The article discusses the legal and ethical implications of allowing a player with a gambling addiction to participate in college football. It highlights the tension between the NCAA's rules and the player's personal struggles. The judge's decision to block the ban has been seen as a significant victory for Sorsby and his supporters, who argue that the player should not be punished for his personal issues.

The article also touches on the broader issue of gambling addiction in sports and the need for better support and resources for players who may be struggling. It raises questions about the role of athletic departments in addressing these issues and the potential consequences for the players and the institution if they are not adequately addressed.
