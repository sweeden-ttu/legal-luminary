The Texas Tribune (Jessica Priest, Ayden Runnels and Alex Nguyen, June 11, 2026) reports that Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton's office warned the Big 12 that it would take legal action if the conference sanctioned Texas Tech for playing quarterback Brendan Sorsby, who admitted placing thousands of sports bets. The Big 12 then sued Paxton and Texas Tech in federal court in Dallas, and a Texas Tech regent later announced Sorsby would not play this season.
