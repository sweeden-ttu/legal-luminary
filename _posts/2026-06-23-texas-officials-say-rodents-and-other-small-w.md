Texas officials are investigating potential sources for New World Screwworm infestations in the area. Previously, cattle were believed to be the primary carriers of the parasite. Current investigations suggest that rodents and other small wildlife may also be responsible for spreading the infestation.

The ongoing investigation aims to determine the true vectors contributing to the spread of the screwworm. This information is crucial for developing effective control measures and public health protocols. Officials are continuing their efforts to identify all potential sources to protect livestock and local agriculture.
