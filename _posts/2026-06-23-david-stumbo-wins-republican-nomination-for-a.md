David Stumbo has secured the Republican nomination for attorney general in South Carolina. This victory concludes a significant phase of the primary election process for the statewide constitutional office.

The position of attorney general involves overseeing the enforcement of state laws and representing the interests of the citizens within the state's legal framework. Those involved in this role are responsible for various aspects of criminal justice and consumer protection.

This nomination marks Stumbo as the Republican candidate moving forward toward the general election. Residents interested in the outcome of the race will be able to follow developments regarding the upcoming statewide contest.
