WIS-TV reports that Upstate solicitor David Stumbo won the June 23, 2026 Republican runoff for South Carolina attorney general, defeating state Sen. Stephen Goldfinch two weeks after trailing him in the initial primary. He will face Democrat Richard Hricik in November.
