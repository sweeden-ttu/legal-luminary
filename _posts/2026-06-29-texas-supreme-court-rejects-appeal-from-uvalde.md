The Texas Tribune's Colleen DeGuzman reports that on June 26, 2026, the Texas Supreme Court declined to hear an appeal from Robb Elementary students and teachers who alleged the Department of Public Safety and Uvalde County were negligent in their response to the 2022 shooting. The decision leaves in place a February ruling by the San Antonio-based 4th Court of Appeals, which held that the Texas Tort Claims Act did not cover the claims.
