State officials are investigating the water consumption practices of large data centers across Texas. These facilities utilize significant amounts of water for cooling their extensive server arrays. A state agency recently distributed a survey designed to quantify this usage among various companies.

However, participation in the required reporting has been low. The survey received responses from less than one-third of the targeted corporations. This lack of comprehensive data raises questions regarding resource management and environmental accountability within the tech industry. Officials are reportedly seeking clearer metrics on water withdrawal for these large industrial operations.
