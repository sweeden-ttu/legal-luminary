The Texas Tribune (Lomi Kriel and Ayden Runnels) reviews video clips from multiple angles showing the moments before an immigration officer fatally shot a Texas man near South Padre Island. The clips omit key audio, but lawyers for the man's family argue they contradict the Trump administration's account that the shooting was self-defense.
