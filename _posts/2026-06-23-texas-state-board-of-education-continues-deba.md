The Texas State Board of Education is currently engaged in a debate regarding proposed changes to state curricula. These proposals suggest significant adjustments to how certain subjects are taught within public schools across the state.

Specifically, the new guidelines advocate for increased instruction concerning civic engagement, patriotism, and Christianity. Proponents argue that these additions will strengthen foundational knowledge among students.

Conversely, the proposal suggests deemphasizing several areas of study currently included in standard curricula. These reduced focuses include world history, cultural studies, diversity topics, and discussions related to race. Officials involved in the debate continue to discuss the legal implications of such major curricular shifts.
