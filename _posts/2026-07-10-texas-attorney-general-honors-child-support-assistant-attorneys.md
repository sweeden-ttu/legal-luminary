The Texas Attorney General recently honored multiple Assistant Attorneys General for their exceptional work in the child support division over the past year. These professionals were recognized for their dedication to ensuring that Texas children receive the financial and medical support they need. The annual awards highlight the state's ongoing commitment to family welfare and the effective enforcement of child support orders across Texas.
