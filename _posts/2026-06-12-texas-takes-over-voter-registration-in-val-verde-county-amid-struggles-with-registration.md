Natalia Contreras of Votebeat and The Texas Tribune reports that the Texas Secretary of State has used a 2023 law, written with Harris County in mind, to take administrative oversight of voter registration in Val Verde County. A state audit cited a recurring pattern of problems, including duplicate records, no written policies, slow processing of provisional ballots and voters assigned to the wrong jurisdictions; county officials said they accept the findings.
