**Headline:** Texas takes over voter registration in Val Verde County amid struggles with registration

**Byline:** Natalia Contreras

**Date:** 2026-06-12T10:00:00+00:00

**Body:**

The secretary of state is using a law that was originally written to focus on problems in Harris County. The Texas Tribune reports that the state is taking over voter registration in Val Verde County, which has been struggling with registration issues. This move comes as the state grapples with voter registration challenges across the board.

The Texas Tribune's Natalia Contreras explains that the law, which was initially designed to address issues in Harris County, is now being used to tackle similar problems in Val Verde County. The state's efforts to streamline and improve voter registration processes are part of a broader effort to ensure that all Texans have access to the polls.

The article highlights the ongoing struggles with voter registration in Val Verde County, a rural area that has seen a decline in registration rates over the past few years. The state's intervention is seen as a crucial step in addressing these challenges and ensuring that all eligible voters can participate in the democratic process.

The Texas Tribune's reporting underscores the importance of accessible and fair voter registration processes, particularly in rural and underserved areas. As the state continues to grapple with these issues, the focus remains on finding solutions that will empower all Texans to have their voices heard.
