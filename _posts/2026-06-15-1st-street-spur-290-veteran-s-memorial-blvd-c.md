According to a City of Temple release, Phase 2 reconstruction of 1st Street (Spur 290) Veteran's Memorial Blvd will close the street to through traffic between Marvin R. Felder Dr. and Avenue R from Monday, June 29, 2026 through approximately January 25, 2027, with traffic detoured to 5th Street. The work includes water line, drainage, roadway and traffic signal reconstruction, and driveway access to the VA Hospital will be maintained via the Main and North gates.
