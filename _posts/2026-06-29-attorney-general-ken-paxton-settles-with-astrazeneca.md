The Office of the Texas Attorney General announced a settlement under which AstraZeneca will pay the state about $34 million ($33,998,000) to resolve claims brought under the Texas Health Care Program Fraud Prevention Act. The state alleged the company used kickbacks, including free nursing help and reimbursement assistance for prescribers, to steer Medicaid-covered prescriptions toward its drugs.
