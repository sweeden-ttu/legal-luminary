Texas lawmakers are requesting that fixes be made to the statewide voter registration system ahead of the midterms. It has been reported that the system has been facing ongoing problems since it was updated last year. Certain lawmakers and election officials are seeking assurances from the Texas Secretary of State's Office that the issues will be resolved prior to the upcoming November elections.
