According to a City of Temple release, the final phase of the Poison Oak Road Improvement Project, affecting Poison Oak Road between Briar Hollow Drive and Old Waco Road, starts Monday, June 22, 2026 and is expected to take about 18 months. The city says the phase will bring a wider, realigned roadway with four lanes, drainage improvements, a new 10-foot sidewalk, street lighting and a 12-inch water line.
