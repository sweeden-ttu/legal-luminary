The Texas Tribune's Alejandro Serrano reports that despite a securities fraud indictment, an impeachment by the Texas House and alleged affairs, Ken Paxton has won every election he has contested, most recently the GOP Senate runoff against Sen. John Cornyn. Paxton now faces Democrat James Talarico in a race polls show as close, and analysts quoted in the article say his legal history could be a liability at the top of the ticket.
