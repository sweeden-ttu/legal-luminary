According to a City of Temple release dated June 22, 2026, the city ordered the temporary closure of the Temple Mall after the expiration of a 30-day notice issued May 22 by the Fire Marshal's office over unresolved fire and life-safety deficiencies. The city cited an inoperable fire pump, impaired sprinkler systems, impaired fire alarm panels and electrical hazards.
